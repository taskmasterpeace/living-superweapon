# AUDIO_SOURCES.md — the provenance ledger

**Every recorded sound in `public/audio/` (300 `.mp3`, ~4 MB, offline-first) traces to a declared
CC0 source here.** This file is the enforceable form of THE AUDIO CONTRACT (audio.js header,
`docs/powerworld/aaa-07-audio.md` §0, POWERWORLD_AAA §1.9):

> Every sound is either a CC0 recording we hold on disk, or DSP we wrote driven by engine-computed
> parameters, usually layered together. **Nothing is ever produced by an AI audio model, and no
> sound is fetched at runtime.**

Assertion 12 of the audio suite (`aaa-07-audio.md` §7.3) checks that every file in `public/audio/`
traces to a row below. A file with no row is either a licence problem or an unattributed asset, and
both are bugs. **All sources are CC0 (public domain dedication);** CC0 imposes no legal attribution
condition, but this ledger exists so provenance is auditable and so no AI-generated asset can enter
the bank unnoticed — the GeoNames precedent (CLAUDE.md: *"ATTRIBUTION IS A LICENCE CONDITION"*),
applied by choice one layer up.

⚠ Nothing here is fetched or generated at build or run time — the files were curated once and are
committed. Adding a family means adding a file **and** a row.

## What stays DSP (no recording, by contract)

These are **generated by us** — the second half of the contract, not a gap. `data/sfx.js` names the
`energy` GRAIN bed `ringmod` precisely so the validator does not flag it as a missing file:

- the ki energy voice (`_ringMod` + inharmonic partials + `_crackle`), the beam voice, the ki charge
  spool — the crafted "power" timbres a generic loop would downgrade;
- the police two-tone siren, the radio filter chain, squelch, the PA hailer;
- the KMK 9 news sting and analogue `staticBurst`;
- the `soundscape` formant SPEECH — no sample library improvises a sentence, and hero voice lines are
  out entirely unless a human records them (`audio.heroVoice` is `false` by default).
