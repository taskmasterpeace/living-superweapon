## Buying and integration decision

First review **one recoverable knockdown family and one rear hold/pickup pair**, not all seven packs. These target the largest current gaps. Boxing is the next worthwhile complete style. Suplex, spear and richer falls are later choices, with overlap reviewed first.

For assets actually licensed under [Fab Standard License](https://www.fab.com/eula), its summary permits compatible tools beyond Unreal and modification within projects, not standalone redistribution. Confirm the selected license and source-format access. An Unreal-only package is not a direct Three.js import; raw FBX availability/export must be verified. Retarget offline to our modular rig, preserve source provenance and paired relative roots, then export selected GLB clips with contact/release/control-return markers. No imported clip automatically supplies combat damage or root physics.

Prices are observation-time “from” values where noted, not a verified combined USD shopping-cart total. Tax, currency, tier and package variants can change the amount. Do not use AssetFreaks membership pricing as creator purchase pricing.
