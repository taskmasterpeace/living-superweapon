v0.8.0 2026-07-25 The voice of the law

The law you can hear before you can see it.

  • New

    Police finally sound like police.

    Every responding vehicle wails continuously instead of chirping once, dispatch calls go out over the radio, and the first officers on scene shout you down through a loudhailer.

  • New

    “Officer down” lands twice.

    The urgent call over the air, and the shout from the man standing right next to him.

  • Improved

    In a corrupt city, you hear the call go nowhere.

    The radio opens, closes, and nobody says a word. The silence is the answer.

v0.7.0 2026-07-24 The Circuit

A career, not a match list.

  • New

    A persistent single-player career.

    Take weekly contracts, travel to real cities, bank purses, build renown and chase the belt. Your record, injuries and ranking carry from one fight to the next.

  • New

    Every offer is a real decision.

    Fight up the rankings for a fatter purse and a genuinely sharper opponent, gamble it all on double-or-nothing, or sit the week out and heal. Win streaks raise your price; a loss resets it.

  • New

    The world fights without you.

    Other fighters trade wins every week, so the rankings you come back to are never the ones you left.

  • New

    Everything has weight.

    Rocks, lamp posts, street trees, cars — and a 24-ton airliner that exactly one strength rank can lift. Throwing a person pits your strength against their body weight.

  • New

    Your character has a homeland.

    Pick a country and hometown in the creator, and the law, the street and the press all treat you differently for it.

v0.6.0 2026-07-24 Low orbit

Leave the city. Then leave the planet.

  • New

    Fly high enough and you can leave.

    A world map of 1,050 real cities, with the trip itself playing as the loading screen.

  • New

    Ten worlds in the solar system.

    Three of them landable, built by the same city engine that makes Earth.

  • New

    Cross the edge of the solar system.

    The view changes to show you the scale of it — your fighter passing the heliopause, then the real distance to the nearest stars.

v0.5.0 2026-07-24 Combat & consequence

The fight got a body.

  • New

    Speed is part of the punch.

    Arriving at a sprint or dropping out of a dive multiplies damage and knockback — and a dive punch drives your target into the ground.

  • New

    Grab someone and throw them at someone else.

    Both of them take it.

  • New

    Wounds that behave like wounds.

    Bleeding that worsens the more you move, injuries that persist between fights, a once-per-match second wind, plus sleep darts and smoke that genuinely blinds.

  • New

    Gear is separate from what you are.

    Drop it, pick up a fallen enemy’s weapon, and use it only as well as your training allows — the same carbine is lethal for a soldier and clumsy for a bruiser.

  • New

    Twenty new powers and afterburners.

    Seven signature upgrades across the roster, and a burner that roughly doubles top flight speed for a fuel cost.

  • Improved

    Plays on phone, tablet and Steam Deck.

    Each gets its own layout rather than a shrunken desktop HUD.

  • Improved

    A rebuilt tutorial covers a real first hour.

    Including getting flagged by the police and living through it.

  • Fixed

    Blocking a beam no longer freezes the game.

    Two separate causes, both found and fixed.

v0.4.0 2026-07-24 ATLAS v1

The map tool became a product.

  • New

    The city builder runs as its own page.

    Paint districts, resize the grid, move the coastline, lock what you like, save named layouts, export and import.

  • New

    Houses you can go inside.

    Fight through them room by room.

  • New

    Water has real depth and forests kill sightlines.

    And the funfair’s wheel actually turns.

v0.3.0 2026-07-23 The world layer

A world worth fighting in.

  • New

    Roads are real.

    Junctions with crosswalks, dead ends with turning circles, meandering dirt tracks and the occasional roundabout — over terrain with hills, valleys and quarries you can fall into.

  • New

    Every city gets the landmarks it’s known for.

    Sized to its population and named after it.

  • New

    All 52 fighters have distinct bodies.

    Measured at 2.4× spread in width, instead of one build in 52 colours.

  • New

    A full sound engine.

    City ambience that shifts by district and time of day, synthesized civilian voices, and separate music, effects and voice faders.

  • Improved

    One visual language across every screen.

    Colours, spacing and type all drawn from a single system.

v0.2.0 2026-07-23 The broadcast update

Somebody is filming.

  • New

    A news crew films your fights.

    A camera operator and reporter chase the action for real, and the post-match broadcast replays their actual footage with an anchor script about what you did and what it cost the city.

  • New

    The Invitational.

    An eight-seed tournament seeded off the live power rankings, in 1v1, 2v2 or underdog formats.

  • New

    1,050 real cities.

    Each generated from its own population, crime and safety figures.

  • New

    Police decide the villain is whoever hurts civilians.

    They escalate through six rungs — beat cops, patrol, tactical, federal agents, the military, and finally one of your own kind.

  • Improved

    Enemies only act on what they’ve seen or heard.

    Break line of sight and you can genuinely lose them.

v0.1.0 2026-07-22 The engine
  • New

    52 playable fighters and a point-buy creator.

    ORIGIN shows you live damage numbers as you spend.

  • New

    A tabletop layer under everything.

    Ranked attributes, talents and carried gear drive the real combat numbers, not just flavour text.

  • New

    Online room-code duels and local two-player.

    Plus an interactive tutorial that watches your real inputs.

  • New

    A city at true human scale.

    A hero is 1.8m against real buildings.
