# Animation integration audit — 2026-09-13

The source assets cover boxing, sword/shield candidates, a forward roll, a supine get-up and basic infected movement. They do **not** supply a verified sideways dive while firing, dedicated bat combat, combat axe swings, or infected flight. Preserve native flight, physics and authored combat while expanding the existing tools.

This is an asset/code audit, not visual acceptance or a gameplay test. ## Minimal wiring path and ownership

**Full skeleton compatibility check:** UAL2 uses UE-style names, not `DEF-*`. All **53** UAL1 skin joints map to UAL2's **65** joints. After anatomical same-side renaming, the maximum local rest-TRS component difference is **0.000001848** and maximum inverse-bind matrix component difference is **0.000002155**. Mapped joint parents agree; the enclosing root object is named `Rig` versus `Armature`. Twelve extra UAL2 leaf joints are ten fingertip leaves and two `ball_leaf` joints. This supports a full-track rename adapter, subject to checking the actual modular target bind, root convention and continuous playback. Map `upperarm_r`→`DEF-upper_arm.R`, `lowerarm_r`→`DEF-forearm.R`, `hand_r`→`DEF-hand.R`, `pelvis`→`DEF-hips`, `spine_01/02/03`→`DEF-spine.001/002/003`, `neck_01`→`DEF-neck`, `Head`→`DEF-head`, `clavicle_r`→`DEF-shoulder.R`, `calf_r`→`DEF-shin.R`, `ball_r`→`DEF-toe.R`; fingers follow `index_01_r`→`DEF-f_index.01.R`, thumb→`DEF-thumb.01.R`, likewise left. Omit unmapped leaf tracks. Do **not** swap anatomical left/right in this full-skeleton path: that convention belongs to the legacy reduced pose bridge.

Existing packaged `roll`/`supine-rise` frames are **45-float reduced poses**, not full skeleton tracks: eight limb-segment directions, five hip/chest/head/feet quaternions and support ratio (`authoring/lib/slots.js`). They omit arm twist and hand/finger rotations. For a shared **modular** registry, prefer original UAL1 clips and mapped full UAL2 tracks, retaining hands and rejecting root trajectory. Do not treat reduced package data as an equivalent source for original modular grip fidelity.

The smallest new source candidate subset is UAL2 `Zombie_Idle_Loop`, `Zombie_Walk_Fwd_Loop`, `Zombie_Scratch`, `Idle_Shield_Loop`, `Shield_OneShot`, `Sword_Regular_A`, `Sword_Regular_A_Rec`, `LayToIdle`, `Hit_Knockback`, plus UAL1 `Roll` already present in the modular source. ## Proposed grounded side-dive input (not implemented)

Use **Sprint held + left/right held + Jump rising edge**, with lateral direction taken from the same active movement basis already used in `controlPlayer`. Read sprint intent directly before firing can suppress the ordinary soldier sprint flag. Require live, supported, on-foot, not flying/gliding, not grabbed, not stunned/frozen, no existing evade/dive and no conflicting committed melee/reload. Simultaneous left+right rejects the chord. Allow firing during the committed dive through native firearm logic, with explicit weapon pose compatibility.

Resolve this chord **before** the `TAP_DIRS` double-tap loop and before assigning `flyHeld`. On a successful dive, consume that jump edge and lateral evade edge, clear that direction's pending tap timestamp, suppress flight takeoff until Jump is released, and do not call `performEvade` again in that frame. On rejection, retain normal jump/flight behavior. Do not repurpose ordinary double-tap evade or crouch-running evade. Share the intent/action resolver with pad and bots. Suggested phases: entry → airborne side dive → supported impact/slide → recover; collision and support determine transitions, animation supplies the body pose. Interruption by knockback or KO cancels the voluntary dive immediately.
