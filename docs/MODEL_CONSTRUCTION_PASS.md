# Model construction pass — 2026-09-06

This is a bounded improvement to the procedural fallback, not a claim that the characters or game meet the creator's final quality bar. No map, roster data, physics, flight input, or camera tuning was changed in this pass.

## Changed

- `hero-rig.js`: close anatomy loft ends with independently shaded caps. Cuffs, necks and bent joints no longer expose an open surface.
- `hero-face.js`: replace glowing human eye rectangles with mirrored eyelids, dark pupils, a shaped bridge, brows and a mouth. Metal/visor paths remain separate. Optic socket positions stay unchanged.
- `figure.js`: fit neck height beneath the independently scaled head; contain its base inside the chest. Keep oversized authored heads above short chests and move their hair by the same clearance adjustment. Open raised collars toward the face, not across it. Keep details on driven meshes.
- `hero-costume.js`: martial V-neck inset and folded lapels, continuous trouser material, shorter wrist wraps. Preserve the emblem slot but hide its generic energy medallion for this costume. Costume reconstruction restores the appropriate visibility.

Impeccable's construction/readability critique guided these edits. Shared material shaders have **not** been overhauled; this is not a completed material-quality pass.

## Still below the target

The bodies still read as segmented procedural figures, especially shoulders, hips and hair. The combat replay still shows a hard bend where a turning emitter meets previously emitted beam energy; the beam's transparent envelope can dominate its read. At the closest crossing the fighters briefly overlap in the camera. None of those gaps is solved by better facial construction. This pass does not establish BFP-level feel, AAA visual quality, completed editor tooling, or final game approval.
