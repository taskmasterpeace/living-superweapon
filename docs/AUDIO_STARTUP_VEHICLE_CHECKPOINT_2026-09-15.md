# Audio startup and vehicle recording checkpoint

The first-use fallback had a concrete startup cause: `AudioBus.init()` prepared only authored SoundLibrary bindings. Bundled `HOT_SET` loading began on the first call to `sample`, `sampleBuffer`, or `sampleLoop`. The bank now starts loading at audio initialization. `await audio.prepareSamples()` returns the same readiness promise on repeated calls, resolving to one buffer per unique hot file, with `null` for failures. This makes a launch readiness check possible without replaying delayed combat events. It does not itself gate match startup, disable fallback, or guarantee success when files fail to load.

`src/data/vehicle-recordings.js` now registers the 11 selected real CC0 vehicle events, 15 files, from `public/audio/sfx-veh/final/veh-winners.json`. Each is preloaded. Only idle is a sustained loop; acceleration remains a transient. Source bytes were compared against `D:/lsw/.worktrees/audio-sfx-gaps` at integration time and matched. `docs/audio/vehicle-recordings-provenance.json` retains source commit, selection path, license and SHA-256 values for portable regression checks.

SoundLibrary defaults are `vehicle-start`, `vehicle-idle`, `vehicle-accel`, `vehicle-off`, `vehicle-door-open`, `vehicle-door-close`, `vehicle-hood`, `vehicle-trunk`, `vehicle-horn`, `vehicle-brake`, and `vehicle-impact`. They map to corresponding `veh.*` samples. Existing authored recordings and explicit placeholder choices retain precedence. The separate movement air-brake cue and vehicle-explosion mapping are unchanged. No rejected `sfx-gaps` recordings were introduced.

The fleet pilot now integrates start, idle, acceleration, moving brake and shutdown for wheeled actors through `src/engine/fleet-audio.js`. The owner updates idle position/intensity, retries an undecoded idle on later active frames, and stops on exit, pause, destruction, death, control transfer, missing occupant, disposal and match reset. Acceleration and moving brake play only on control transitions. Stale one-shots are not queued. Selected user recordings remain authoritative; explicit placeholder choices still reach SoundLibrary. Other motion classes never acquire the car recordings.

Legacy scout driving is separate and still needs these lifecycle hooks. Vehicle impact needs a reliable collision or accepted damage signal: `driveActor` currently zeros motion for cover, arena bounds and water refusal alike, and its fleet actor has no damage hook. Do not guess a collision from stopping alone. Doors, hood, trunk and horn need corresponding gameplay events. Aircraft engine, reload and hover content gaps are unchanged. Existing footsteps, selected physical combat and travel flight mappings remain intact.

`tools/fleet-audio.test.mjs` plus `tools/fleet-pilot.test.mjs`: 24 tests passed, including actual pilot transitions, SoundLibrary buffer routes, cold decode, authored preferences, loop ownership/cleanup and original movement checks for five vehicle classes.

Verification uses mocked WebAudio graph behavior, actual file hashes, source preference checks and decoding readiness tests. No in-game listening review is claimed.
