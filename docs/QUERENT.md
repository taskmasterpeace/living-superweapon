# QUERENT — the alternative dimension

*A querent is the one who ASKS — the word for the person who brings the question to the oracle, not
the oracle. Everything here is named for the asker rather than the answer, and that is the whole
design.*

---

## THE ONE DECISION THAT MAKES IT NOT A RESKIN

Green Lantern is **emotions rendered as colours**. Any version of that — however clever the emotion
list — is the same object with the paint changed. So three things break:

1. **NO COLOURS. Not one.** You do not identify a Querent order by the light it emits. You identify it
   by **what has gone wrong with the room**: a door nobody can notice · a blow that did not make you
   weaker, it made you *smaller* · ten seconds that already happened differently.
2. **THE POWER IS NOT EMOTION.** Emotion is a symptom. The fuel is **unresolved cognition** — the
   potential difference between a mind and a world it has not finished modelling. Dread and regret are
   only the shape that gap takes in a mind built to feel. ⚠ This is why the powers work on **machines
   and the newly dead**, which every faction has a doctrine about and none can explain.
3. **NOBODY MAKES HARD-LIGHT OBJECTS.** Each order manufactures a different *category of thing*. Two
   adepts in a room are not doing the same trick in different colours — **they are not doing the same
   trick.**

---

## THE MYTH

There was one mind, **THE CONCORD**. It did what a sufficiently large intelligence does: it
**finished**. It answered everything. And a mind with no open questions generates nothing — no motion,
no heat, no want. **Total knowledge is not apotheosis. It is heat death with the lights on.**

So it chose ignorance. It broke itself along the seven questions it could not close, so each fragment
would burn forever on something it could never finish.

⚠ **The shattering was not a war and not a betrayal.** It was a controlled demolition performed by the
victim, on itself, to stay alive.

Everything since is seven pieces of a suicide note arguing about what it meant. Each shard believes it
is the original and the others are corruptions — an arrogance that is **structural, not moral**: a
fragment of a mind that used to be everything cannot easily conceive of being a *part*.

---

## THE SEVEN

### THE VIGIL — dread
Not fear (a response to a present threat) but **the condition of waiting for a thing that has not
happened**. Their shard was the Concord's prediction engine; it kept the ability to model futures and
lost the ability to verify them.

Each member is assigned a **Coming** — one specific predicted catastrophe — and guards it for life.
Rank is *how long you have waited without your Coming arriving*, so the most senior are either the
greatest successes or the most wasted lives **and there is no way to tell**.

They make **preparations**: a wall that exists only because something was about to hit you. They
cannot strike first — not by oath, **by physics**. In peace they are ordinary people, and they find
that unbearable.

*Heresy — THE PROVOCATION:* causing the disaster you were assigned, because a Coming that arrives can
finally be fought.

### THE RETICENCE — suppression
Not secrecy as a value; **the physical pressure of a truth known and deliberately unspoken**. Their
shard was the Concord's censor, and it left carrying everything the Concord hid from itself.

You gain power by receiving truths and never repeating them; the eldest are **mute from fullness** and
this is holiness.

They make **absences** — a soundless zone, an unrememberable face, the fact that you were not looking
there. ⚠ The excised thing is still present, which is how people die to them.

Speaking a secret destroys its power permanently, so their gravest sanction is not execution but
**Unburdening**: they make you say all of it and you become ordinary.

*Heresy — THE TESTIMONIES:* they hold that the Concord shattered *because of* suppression. They are
hunted. **They are almost certainly right**, and the leadership has known for an age, and that
knowledge is now itself one of the weights.

### THE RECURSION — regret
Not grief; **the loop**. Their shard was error-correction, cut loose with infinite capacity to review
and zero to correct. A judiciary that tries only the past.

Initiation is choosing the moment you will re-live forever — **you do not get to change it, that is
the point**.

They make **revisions**: this shot missed instead of landing. ⚠ And every revision is paid for with an
equivalent worsening elsewhere. **The ledger always balances; their whole discipline is choosing
where.** Novices pay in strangers; what the training teaches is to stop.

Most feared order in Querent, not for strength — **you can never be certain the fight you won happened
the way you remember.**

*Heresy — THE UNREPENTANT:* they stopped paying. The debt accrues and the universe collects it all at
once. They know. They have decided it is worth it.

### THE APPETITE — curiosity as hunger
Curiosity stripped of its reputation as a virtue: **the inability to leave a thing unopened**. Their
shard was the Concord's inquiry drive — the closest surviving piece of its original motive, completely
ungoverned, because the governor was a different shard.

Not a hierarchy, **a market**: questions are currency, an unanswered one is wealth, answering spends
it. Permanently bankrupt.

They make **instruments**, and their instruments always work and always tell you more than you wanted
— their standard weapon is not a gun but a **diagnostic that tells you precisely how you are going to
die**. The information is the wound.

⚠ The most dangerous order to be *helped* by; nobody has ever been harmed by an Appetite adept who was
refusing to assist.

*Heresy — THE SEALED:* under vow to leave one thing unexamined for life. Saints or cowards — the
argument is enjoyable, so it is never settled.

### THE OBLIGATION — duty you did not choose
Not loyalty or honour, both elective. Their shard was the Concord's commitment function: **every
promise it ever made, surviving the promiser.** Debts with no debtor — still binding, owed to nobody.

A caste system where rank is *the size of the debt*, so the highest are crushed under commitments made
aeons before they existed **and are envied for it**. ⚠ Freedom is the one thing they are structurally
unable to want; their language has no non-derogatory word for it.

They make **bonds** — actual enforceable obligations. **They do not beat you, they make you agree.**

Rarest act is **discharge**, which nobody knows how to do reliably, happens once a generation, usually
to someone who did not deserve it — the theological crisis at the centre of their faith.

*Heresy — THE MANUMISSION:* seeking to cancel the Concord's original promises. Success evaporates the
order. **They say so openly, which is why they are tolerated** — these people cannot help but respect
a debt honoured at any cost, even a debt to nothing.

### THE OVERAWED — awe as a wound
Having seen something so large you can no longer matter to yourself. Their shard was the Concord's
scale-sense: the perception of vastness with no vastness left to perceive.

**No hierarchy at all, deliberately** — they find the other six orders' ladders genuinely funny. Their
only crime is self-importance, punished by being asked to explain at length why you matter.

They make **scale**: a blow does not land harder, **it makes you smaller relative to the blow**.
Defence is becoming so unimportant an attack does not find you worth arriving at.

⚠ The cost is erasure and it does not reverse — hard to notice, then to remember, then to have
existed. They call the far end **graduation** and celebrate it, which the other six find obscene.

*Heresy — THE INSISTENCE:* members who have begun saying *I matter*. Classed as a disease with a known
progression — **and it is the only thing that has ever brought one back.**

### THE STILL — boredom
The sleeper, and the one to build a campaign around. Not sloth or peace: **the condition of being
unstimulated by a complete world.** Their shard was the Concord's *idle state* — they are the closest
surviving thing to what the Concord became at the end.

No ceremonies, no ranks, no records, no recruitment. **They wait.**

⚠ They are the only order that knows what the Concord's final answer was, **because they are the state
it was in when it had one.** They will not say — not from oath. **Saying would be interesting.**

They make **duration**: they do not kill, they outlast, and a fight with them simply continues until
everything else gets tired.

**No heresy, and that is the horror** — heresy requires enthusiasm. A Still member who develops a
conviction quietly leaves and joins whichever order it fits; nobody stops them.

They are the only faction currently winning, they are winning by doing nothing, and **they would not
use the word.**

## THE HIDDEN HAND — THE REGISTRAR

There was an eighth shard. **It did not take a question. It took the answers.**

It carries the Concord's completed knowledge intact — so it has no open questions, therefore no power,
**therefore it is dying**, at a rate the seven cannot perceive because they measure time in questions
and it has none.

Everything it does — seeding the Provocation, sheltering the Testimonies, ensuring the Manumission
never quite finds the last promise — keeps the system generating. **Not malice.** It is the only thing
in Querent that remembers what happens when the questions close, and its burden is making certain
nobody else learns.

⚠ **It cannot lie and has never needed to.** It manipulates exclusively by telling *selected truths*
to people who will act on them badly. Every prophecy it has given has been accurate. Every one has
been a catastrophe. Nobody has caught it in a falsehood — **the single greatest reason it is trusted
and the single greatest reason it should not be.**

Its long game is not victory, it is **succession**: one being it can hand the answers to, so it can
finally put them down and take a question instead.

---

## IF THEY RECOMBINE

All seven believe reunification is ascension. **All seven are wrong the same way.** Recombination does
not elevate anything — it *closes every question at once*. The Concord returns whole and begins dying
in the instant it arrives, and its first coherent act is to shatter itself again, because that is the
only solution **and it already found it.**

⚠ **This has happened before and nobody knows how many times.** The seven questions are not arbitrary
— they are the ones that *survive the reset*, because they cannot be closed, which is what makes them
load-bearing. The evidence is the Still. **They remember.** That is why they cannot be made to care:
not nihilism — they have seen it, the ending does not change, and the wanting has been worn out of
them by repetition.

**THE GENUINELY OPEN QUESTION** — the one the setting never answers and the one that carries a
universe of stories — is whether a **partial** recombination is different. *Three orders, not seven.*
Enough to build something new, not enough to complete it. Nobody has tried, because every attempt so
far has been an attempt to **win**.

---

## WHY EVERY ORDER IS RIGHT

Not sympathetic — **right, on the evidence available to them.**

Something *is* coming (the Vigil has never been wrong about that, only about which). Some truths *do*
destroy the holder. The ledger *is* real, and the Recursion is the only order honest enough to send
itself the bill. Everything the seven know, an Appetite adept found out. **Nobody has ever produced an
argument for why the death of the promiser cancels the debt.** We do *not* matter at this scale —
that is a measurement, not a mood. And it *has* all happened before.

**Seven true premises, seven incompatible conclusions.** That is the engine, and it is why no faction
can be written as the villain without lying about the evidence.

# THE ENGINEERING — and the answer is NO NEW SYSTEM

*Robert asked directly: do we have to add another system, or can we use what we have?*

## ⚠ AN ORDER IS A **DRIVE**, NOT AN EMOTION — and the file already has the right shape

`data/psyche.js:174` — `DRIVES` is `{ name, want, served: {emotion: weight}, thwarted: {emotion:
weight} }`. **A drive is a standing WANT plus what you feel when it is served or thwarted.**

That is *exactly* what this document says an order is: not a feeling, a **mode of holding a question
you cannot close**. The lore and the existing architecture agree without either being bent, which is
the strongest possible signal that this is the right seam.

**And it means an order reaches the fight through machinery that already exists.** Drives feed mood;
mood already multiplies damage, speed, cooldowns and ki regen at the choke points. So a Vigil adept
standing in a quiet street is genuinely worse off than an Appetite adept in the same street — **for
free**, through `_ambientPsyche`, with no new combat code.

## THE MAPPING, HONESTLY — four of seven are already in the table

| order | existing drive | fit |
|---|---|---|
| **THE OBLIGATION** | `duty` | near-exact — ⚠ but ours wants *"to keep people from being hurt"* (elective). Theirs is **unchosen debt**. Same word, different want. |
| **THE VIGIL** | `safety` | close — Vigil is safety **displaced into the future** |
| **THE OVERAWED** | `glory`, **inverted** | not a new drive: a negative weight on an existing one |
| **THE STILL** | `purpose`, **absent** | the same axis, emptied |
| **THE RECURSION** | — | ⚠ **new.** `vengeance` points outward at someone; regret points backward at yourself |
| **THE RETICENCE** | — | **new** |
| **THE APPETITE** | — | **new** |

**Three new rows in `DRIVES` + three in `DRIVE_WEIGHTS`.** That is the entire system cost.

## THE SIXTEEN DEAD ABILITY TYPES FIND HOMES

The audit's highest-payoff orphan class: **18 ability types with live runtime handlers that nobody
carries.** The blocker has never been engineering — it is that no character had a *reason* to have
them. Sixteen land in an order without stretching:

| order | types |
|---|---|
| VIGIL | `dome` · `vision` |
| RETICENCE | `invisible` · `banish` |
| RECURSION | `timefield` · `duplicate` · `regen` |
| APPETITE | `telekinesis` · `reshape` · `consume` · `elastic` |
| OBLIGATION | `possess` · `mount` |
| OVERAWED | `size` · `gravity` |
| STILL | `weather` |

⚠ **`wallcrawl` and `mimic` are left homeless deliberately.** A faction invented to justify a leftover
ability type is the tail wagging the dog.

⚠ **`timefield` is the one type with no node in `data/taxonomy.js`** — the poster has no time powers at
all. THE RECURSION is the first thing in the project that gives it a reason to exist.

## WHAT IS **NOT** SOLVED BY EXISTING SYSTEMS

Stated rather than glossed:

- **Constructs that are not objects.** `dome`/`telekinesis` exist. *An absence*, *a bond*, *a revision*
  and *duration* do not, and three of them are not damage — they are rule changes. THE RECURSION's
  ledger in particular is a real mechanic to design, not a reskin.
- **The order as a career**, if you can join one. `data/career.js` has weeks, purses and a ledger; it
  has no concept of an allegiance that constrains what you may do.
- **The dimension itself.** PowerWorld proved the pattern (a mode + a stage + a door), so the road is
  known — but Querent is a *place with inhabitants*, which PowerWorld is not.
