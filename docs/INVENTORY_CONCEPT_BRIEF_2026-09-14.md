# Power World inventory concept brief

Purpose: make equipped weapons, signature abilities and carried items immediately understandable. Current primary/secondary buttons select combat actions; they are not proof of two physical weapon holsters. Current inventory is a text-heavy modal with gadgets and a Soldier armory link; no spatial backpack grid is implemented.

Concept A — Loadout Board: a Soldier example, central current-style faceted character, explicit primary weapon, sidearm and melee/sheath cards, two quick-use gadgets, and a small backpack grid demonstrating small and long item footprints. The grid is a proposed design, not a live capacity rule. Display ammunition, carried weight/capacity and item dimensions separately; physical lift strength does not give infinite backpack space. Power abilities remain a separate panel from equipment.

Concept B — Field Kit: a weapon-based Living Superweapon example. Show signature pistols/sword as assigned kit, separate primary/secondary action bindings, two gadget slots and a compact comparison drawer. No general backpack or unrestricted rifle slot. Prefer large silhouettes and readable numbers over inventory Tetris. This illustrates the user's intended signature-weapon exception without changing the existing equipment policy.

Both: Power World branding, graphite surfaces, warm gold headings/active state, cream text, sparse cyan energy information, faceted low-poly asset thumbnails. Reserve red for damage/restrictions. Avoid purple. Icons are transparent item artwork independent of slot background; long weapons can use wider display cards while keeping a standard square master. Labels and state badges remain HTML/UI text in implementation, not baked into icon pixels.

Concept images are design previews. They do not establish implemented slots, actual loadout capacity, finished icons, real item weights or expanded equipment permissions. Production values must come from canonical item data. Concepts are generated with the built-in image tool; exact prompts are recorded alongside the output references in the completion report.
