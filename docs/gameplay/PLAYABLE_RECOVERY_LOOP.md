# Field recovery loop — current playable rules

This is the current operation-local research loop. Completing research is not yet the operation's victory condition; convoy objectives still determine the operation outcome.

1. Choose your character and squad. In the Threat Room, use the ready interaction and walk through the portal to deploy.
2. Walk up the transport's rear ramp and press E when SIT appears. Wait for living squad members to board. Enter starts the automatic depot route; Z changes seats. The pilot seat does not enable manual flight. J exits only while parked and asks the squad to disembark too.
3. Defeat an enemy who can supply a biological sample. Friendly fighters, metal characters, training dummies and the operation scientist do not supply research blood. Look toward the dropped vial and press E when COLLECT appears.
4. The sample lasts 90 seconds from its drop, including travel time. Reboard and use Enter to return to the lab. Follow the sample timer in the existing objective panel. Death or complete degradation loses the carried sample.
5. Exit, approach the lab door and face it. Press E when OPEN appears. Walk inside, turn toward the research terminal and press E when ANALYZE appears. Being near an interaction is not enough if you are looking away from it.
6. Analysis consumes one fresh sample and adds 10 maximum energy for this operation, up to three upgrades. It does not refill current energy, create replacement reserves, or permanently unlock a power. Return to combat to use the increased capacity.

## Current limits

The recorded transport test uses staged low-health enemies and initial placement to isolate the complete mechanical route. It does not prove the encounter balance, unaided discoverability, controller/touch route, or all terrain/parking variations. The creator's parked-jeep obstruction remains tracked under GitHub #6 until its specific reproduction is verified. The transport's return ramp now faces the lab, reducing the detour around its hull.
