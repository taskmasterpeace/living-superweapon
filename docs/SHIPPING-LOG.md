# 📣 WAR WORLD: ASCENDANTS — What's New

**119 updates · 8 releases · 4 active days · last shipped today**
Built in public. Everything below is on `main` at [taskmasterpeace/living-superweapon](https://github.com/taskmasterpeace/living-superweapon) — currently **v0.8.0**, pre-release.

> An isometric superhero fighting game where the world is the opponent too: 52 fighters, 1,050 real
> cities, a police force that decides you're the villain, and a news crew that films the whole thing.

---

## v0.8.0 — July 25, 2026
*The law you can hear before you can see it.*

### 🆕 New
- **Police sound like police.** Every responding vehicle wails continuously instead of chirping
  once, dispatch calls go out over the radio, and the first officers on scene shout you down
  through a loudhailer.
- **"Officer down" lands twice** — the urgent call over the air, and the shout from the man
  standing next to him.

### ✨ Improved
- **In a corrupt city you hear the call go nowhere:** the radio opens, closes, and nobody says
  anything. The silence is the answer.

## v0.7.0 — July 24, 2026
*A career, not a match list.*

### 🆕 New
- **THE CIRCUIT — a persistent single-player career.** Take weekly contracts, travel to real
  cities, bank purses, build renown and chase the belt. Your record, your injuries and your
  ranking carry from one fight to the next.
- **Every offer is a real decision.** Fight up the rankings for a fatter purse and a genuinely
  sharper opponent, gamble the whole purse on double-or-nothing, or sit the week out and heal.
  Win streaks raise your price; a loss resets it.
- **The world fights without you.** Other fighters trade wins every week, so the rankings you
  come back to are never the ones you left.
- **Everything has weight.** Rocks, lamp posts, street trees, cars — and a 24-ton airliner that
  exactly one strength rank can lift. Throwing a *person* is your strength against their body
  weight, so a titan hurls a gunman across the block and the gunman can barely shove him.
- **Your character has a homeland.** Pick a country and hometown in the creator and the law, the
  street and the press all treat you differently for it.

## v0.6.0 — July 24, 2026
*Leave the city. Then leave the planet.*

### 🆕 New
- **Fly high enough and you can leave.** A world map of 1,050 real cities, with the trip itself
  playing as the loading screen.
- **Ten worlds in the solar system**, three of them landable, built by the same city engine that
  makes Earth.
- **Cross the edge of the solar system** and the view changes to show you the scale of it — your
  fighter passing the heliopause, then the real distance to the nearest stars.

## v0.5.0 — July 24, 2026
*The fight got a body.*

### 🆕 New
- **Speed is part of the punch.** Arriving at a sprint or dropping out of a dive multiplies the
  damage and the knockback — and a dive punch drives your target into the ground.
- **Grab someone and throw them at someone else.** Both of them take it.
- **Wounds that behave like wounds:** bleeding that gets worse the more you move, injuries that
  persist between fights, a second wind that lets you rise once per match with an empty tank,
  plus sleep darts and smoke that genuinely blinds.
- **Gear is separate from what you are.** Drop it, pick up a fallen enemy's weapon, and use it
  only as well as your training allows — the same carbine is lethal for a soldier and clumsy for
  a bruiser.
- **Twenty new powers, seven signature upgrades, and afterburners** that roughly double your top
  flight speed for a fuel cost.

### ✨ Improved
- **Plays on phone, tablet and Steam Deck**, each with its own layout rather than a shrunken
  desktop HUD.
- **A rebuilt tutorial** walks you through a real first hour — including getting flagged by the
  police and living through it.
- **Real recorded sound effects** for every impact, footstep and interface click.

### 🐛 Fixed
- **Blocking a beam no longer freezes the game.** Two separate causes, both found and fixed.

## v0.3.0 — July 23, 2026
*A world worth fighting in.*

### 🆕 New
- **Roads are real** — junctions with crosswalks, dead ends with turning circles, dirt tracks
  that meander, and the occasional roundabout — over terrain with hills, valleys and quarries
  you can actually fall into.
- **Every city gets the landmarks it's known for**, sized to its population and named after it.
- **All 52 fighters have distinct bodies** — measured at 2.4× spread in width — instead of one
  build in 52 colours.
- **A full sound engine:** city ambience that changes by district and time of day, synthesized
  civilian voices, and separate music, effects and voice faders.

### ✨ Improved
- **One visual language** across every screen in the game.

## v0.2.0 — July 23, 2026
*The broadcast update.*

### 🆕 New
- **A news crew films your fights.** A camera operator and reporter chase the action for real,
  and the post-match broadcast replays their actual footage with an anchor script about what you
  did and what it cost the city.
- **The Invitational** — an eight-seed tournament seeded off the live power rankings, in 1v1,
  2v2 or underdog formats.
- **1,050 real cities**, each generated from its own population, crime and safety figures.
- **Police decide the villain is whoever hurts civilians**, and escalate through six rungs — beat
  cops, patrol, tactical, federal agents, the military, and finally one of your own kind.

### ✨ Improved
- **Enemies only act on what they've seen or heard.** No more opponents walking straight at you
  through a wall — break line of sight and you can genuinely lose them.
