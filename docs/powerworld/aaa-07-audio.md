# AAA-07 — THE SOUND OF 364 POWERS

**WAR WORLD: ASCENDANTS / POWERWORLD — the audio spec.**
Written 2026-07-27. Everything about our engine is cited `file:line`. Everything about BFP or JKA is
cited to the research doc and its own line — **and where the research is silent, this document says
so instead of inventing a citation.**

The design thesis this serves, verbatim from Robert:

> *"a stylistic third-person shooter where BFP is in the air and Jedi Knight is on the ground.
> Leave the ground and you are playing Bid for Power. Touch it and you are playing Jedi Academy.
> The camera never changes hands, the reticle never lies, and every one of the 52 fighters gets
> both grammars with no exceptions."*

Altitude is the mode switch. **Audio has to switch with it, and today it does not switch at all** —
`audio.listen(x, z)` (`game.js:3592`) ignores Y entirely, so a fight 900 units above a city hears the
city at full strength. That is §6.

## 0. ⚠ THE CONTRADICTION, RESOLVED — READ THIS FIRST

Two rulings appear to collide.

| When | Ruling |
|---|---|
| 2026-07-24 | *"get audio for ALL sound effects and powers — no more generated stuff"* (`samples.js:8-9`) |
| 2026-07-26 | *"100% should be wav/mp3, no coded sound effects for any attacks"* (`samples.js:126-128`, CLAUDE.md § AUDIO: EVERY ATTACK IS A RECORDING) |
| **Tonight** | *"generated sounds for all attacks no ai sounds"* |

**The working reading in the brief is: every attack is built from the existing CC0 RECORDED bank plus
our own DSP shaping, and nothing is ever produced by an AI audio generator.**

### THE READING HOLDS. It is not a compromise — it is literally what `gunshot()` already does.

I checked it against the code rather than against memory. `audio.gunshot()` (`audio.js:816-871`) is
the proof, and it is the single most successful audio thing in this repo:

- **The transient is a RECORDING.** `this.sample('gun.crack', …)` (`audio.js:834`) plays
  `impactPlate_heavy_*` — a real recorded plate impact — pitched by the weapon's own `crack` figure.
- **The body, tail and action are GENERATED** on top of it (`audio.js:843-870`) from four scalars per
  weapon (`armory.js:28-40`).
- Result: **13 firearms, 13 signatures, zero AI, zero per-weapon assets.**

"Generated" in tonight's phrasing means *synthesised by us from parameters* — the opposite of
*fetched from a generative model*. The three rulings are one ruling seen from three sides:

> **THE AUDIO CONTRACT.** Every sound in this game is either (a) a CC0 recording we hold on disk, or
> (b) DSP we wrote, driven by parameters the engine already computes, usually layered on top of (a).
> **Nothing is ever generated by an AI audio model, and no sound is ever fetched at runtime.**

That is the line to put in `docs/COMBAT_MANUAL.md` and in the header of `core/audio.js`.

### ⚠ THE ONE PLACE THE PROJECT IS ACTUALLY IN BREACH OF "NO AI SOUNDS"

It is not in `audio.js`. It is in **`docs/VOICE_AND_SFX.md`**, which is still the audio bible on
paper and which says, in its own words:

> `docs/VOICE_AND_SFX.md:3-4` — *"Voice lines are written to be generated per-character with
> country-appropriate accents (Ad Lab voice pipeline)."*
> `docs/VOICE_AND_SFX.md:66` — *"(…every remaining hero gets the same 8 slots; generate with
> `generate-voice.js` per accent.)"*

`generate-voice.js` is the **Ad Lab AI TTS script** (global CLAUDE.md, § Ad Lab v2.0). That is a
plan to ship 52 heroes × 8 barks of AI-generated speech, and tonight's ruling kills it. The engine
never implemented it (nothing in `src/` calls it), so nothing has to be ripped out — but the document
must be corrected in the same loop, or the next person to read the bible will build the banned thing.

**Ruling to record:** hero voice lines are OUT unless recorded by a human. The engine already
defaults to that position — `audio.heroVoice` is **false** by default (`audio.js:46`), and
`yell/grunt/cry` all gate on it (`audio.js:254, 280, 293`). Robert's 2026-07-24 "NO LSW TALKING"
ruling and tonight's "no AI sounds" ruling agree. The rest of `VOICE_AND_SFX.md` — the SFX manifest
table at its foot — is *stale in the other direction*: it lists as "needed" a dozen sounds the sample
bank shipped two days later.

## 3. THE DEFECT LEDGER — five real bugs, found by reading, ranked by damage

### ⚠ D1 — **THE WATCHDOG KILLS EVERY RECORDED LOOP IN THE GAME. UNIT MISMATCH.**

This is the biggest audio defect in the repo and it is one line.

`AudioBus.sweep()` reaps stale sustained handles (`audio.js:50-54`):

```js
sweep() {
  if (!this._sus.size) return;
  const now = performance.now();                                  // MILLISECONDS
  for (const h of this._sus) if (now - h.last > 450) h.stop();
}
```

Every **synthesised** handle stamps `last` in the same unit — `charge()` at `audio.js:398`,
`beamVoice()` at `:506`, `sustain()` at `:704` all use `performance.now()`. Correct.

**`SampleBank.loop()` does not.** `samples.js:192-198`:

```js
const T = () => a.ctx.currentTime;                                // SECONDS
…
const h = {
  last: T(),                                                      // ← seconds
  set(I, p) { h.last = T(); …                                     // ← seconds, every frame
```

`performance.now()` counts from navigation start; `ctx.currentTime` counts from AudioContext
creation, which happens on the first user gesture. **After 450 ms of page life the difference is
permanently greater than 450**, so `sweep()` — called unconditionally every frame from
`game.js:3581`, *"even on title/pause"* — stops every recorded loop on the frame after it is created.

`loop().stop()` (`samples.js:203-207`) sets `_dead`, deletes the handle from `_sus`, fades the gain
and schedules `src.stop(T() + 0.4)`. After 0.4 s **the buffer source is permanently stopped** and no
amount of `set()` can revive it — but `set()` keeps returning normally, so the caller believes the
loop is playing.

**What is broken, right now, in every match:**

| Loop | Owner | Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| `engine.charge` (`audio.js:363`) | every `beam`, `charge`, `growingorb`, `facebomb`, `nova` — **55 slots** | The charge spool-up plays for ~0.4 s then goes silent while you hold |
| `engine.low` (`audio.js:477`) | every `BeamHose` — **25 beams** | The beam voice dies immediately; the whole `set(intensity)` strain mechanic is inaudible |
| `fire.roar` (`audio.js:602`) | 25 fire cones | A flamethrower makes a 0.4 s noise |

⚠ **And this bug was CREATED by the fix that was meant to honour the recording ruling.** Before
`engine.charge`/`engine.low` were added to `HOT_SET` (`samples.js:129`, dated 2026-07-26), the
buffers were not decoded on first use, `sampleLoop` returned `null`, and every caller fell through to
the **synth** — which stamps `last` correctly and works. Making the loops recorded is what handed
them to the broken watchdog. This is precisely the failure mode `audio.js:26-36` documents for the
*other* half of the same handle contract (`ramp` vs `set`), one commit earlier, in the same file.

**Fix.** One clock. `samples.js` should stamp `performance.now()` like every other handle:

```js
const T  = () => a.ctx.currentTime;      // for AudioParam scheduling — must stay ctx time
const NOW = () => performance.now();     // for the watchdog — must match audio.js:52
…
last: NOW(),
set(I, p) { h.last = NOW(); … }
```

⚠ **Do not "fix" it by widening the 450 ms window** — the two clocks diverge without bound, so no
threshold works. And **do not** convert `sweep()` to ctx time: `performance.now()` keeps running when
the AudioContext is suspended, which is what makes the watchdog able to reap a loop whose owner died
during a pause.

**Regression guard** (§7): create a recorded loop, step 60 sim frames driving `set()` every frame,
assert the handle is still in `audio._sus` and its gain is above the floor. That assertion fails
today.

amount of improving `_pg` reaches these 77 calls.

### ⚠ D3 — `blast()` called with the wrong signature at three sites; latent throw into the frame loop

`blast(freq, dur, type, pos)` (`audio.js:141`). Three callers pass a position as the **third**
argument, where the oscillator type belongs:

| Site | Call |
|---|---|
| `abilities.js:530` | `g.audio.blast(140, 0.5, c.pos)` — the `weather` ultimate |
| `game.js:1055` | `this.audio.blast(120, 0.5, pos)` |
| `systems.js:383` | `this.g.audio.blast(90, 0.4, pos)` |

Two consequences, and the second is serious:

1. `pos` is `null`, so all three play at full gain from anywhere (D2 again).
2. If the sample ever misses, execution reaches `o.type = type` (`audio.js:145`) with a `Vector3`.
   **`OscillatorNode.type` throws a `TypeError` on an invalid value** — an exception inside the frame
   loop, which is the one audio law this project holds absolutely (`audio.js:18-20`). It is latent
   only because `ki.blast` is in `HOT_SET` (`samples.js:119`) and the early return at `audio.js:143`
   fires first. **A cold cache, an audio 404, or a decode failure makes it live.**

Fix both by making `blast` accept `(freq, dur, posOrType, pos)` and sniff — or, better and in keeping
with the rest of the file, coerce: `if (type && typeof type === 'object') { pos = type; type = 'sawtooth'; }`.

## 5. THE POWER VOICE — the `VOICES` pattern, generalised

### 5.1 The problem, in one number

**MATERIAL alone cannot carry this.** Measured over the live roster via `visOf`:

```
MATERIAL: energy 284 · fire 19 · steel 17 · ice 8 · air 8 · shock 7 · toxic 5 · shadow 5
          arcane 5 · light 4 · acid 2                                    (364 total)
```

**78% of every ability in the game is `energy`.** Keying the sound on material puts 284 slots in one
bucket — the identical mistake `visual.js:189-192` documents for beams (*"seven hand-picked buckets
put eleven of the twenty-five beams in the same one — the same 'a ladder must come from the
distribution' mistake this project has now made four times"*), and CLAUDE.md records as having been
made **five** times since.

`visual.js`'s answer is the right one and I am copying it exactly: **orthogonal axes that multiply.**

### 5.2 THE VOICE VECTOR — four layers and four scalars

A power's voice is the same shape as a firearm's, because a firearm *is* a power in this engine
(`rifle` is an ability type). One structure, two producers.

```
                    FIREARM (today)              POWER (proposed)
  transient    →    crack   (recorded plate)     ATTACK  — recorded family from SHAPE
  fundamental  →    body    (Hz)                 BODY    — Hz, from MASS × caster rank
  decay        →    tail    (s)                  TAIL    — s, from RANGE + BLAST + residue
  texture      →    mech    (0..1 action noise)  GRAIN   — recorded family from MATERIAL, 0..1
```

Every one of the eight fields is **derived from data the engine already computes.** Nothing new is
authored, and `def.sfx = {…}` overrides any field — the same law as `def.vis` (`visual.js:280`).

#### AXIS 1 — **ATTACK**, from `visOf().shape`. The silhouette decides the transient.

SHAPE is the trait `visual.js:15` says *"carries the grayscale test"*, and it is the closest
available analogue to *what arrives first*. Its distribution is far healthier than material's:

```
field 71 · arc 63 · bolt 59 · fist 58 · cone 36 · orb 28 · hose 26
burst 12 · chain 6 · shell 2 · blade 2 · rift 1
```

| shape | ATTACK family | Bank name | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fist` | punch | `punch.med` / `punch.heavy` by mass | it is a fist |
| `bolt` | small discharge | `ki.blast` | the existing projectile voice |
| `orb` | large discharge | `ki.release` | mass leaving the hand |
| `hose` | ignition | `fire.roar` head + `engine.low` sustain | a beam has no impact, it has an **onset** |
| `cone` | exhale | `swing.air` | the burst of pressure, then the sustain |
| `burst` | detonation | `boom` (+ `boom.deep` above mass 0.85) | already correct in `audio.boom` |
| `blade` | steel | `swing.blade` | |
| `shell` | thrown object | `chop` / `debris.wood` | a canister is a *thunk*, not a zap |
| `chain` | whip | `swing.air` + `armor.shift` | tentacles and grapples |
| `field` | cast | `cast.spell` / `cast.magic` | buffs, domes, auras |
| `arc` | displacement | `fx.glitch` | teleports, dashes, blinks |
| `rift` | tear | `fx.forcefield` + `fx.glitch` | **the portal fix** |

⚠ **Every family named above already exists in `MANIFEST` (`samples.js:23-114`). Zero new assets.**

#### AXIS 2 — **GRAIN**, from `visOf().material`. What it is made of decides the texture.

Material is a *bad* primary axis and a *good* secondary one, because the 80 non-energy slots are
exactly the ones that most need to be told apart from the energy mass.

| material | GRAIN bed | Filter shaping | Bank name |
|---|---|---|---|
| `energy` | ring-mod shimmer | — | (DSP, `_ringMod` `audio.js:319`) |
| `fire` | roar | LP opening with intensity | `fire.roar` |
| `ice` | crystal ticks | HP 4–8 kHz | `glass.light` |
| `toxic` | hiss | BP 3.2 kHz + 5.5 Hz tremolo | `ped.breath` looped |
| `acid` | sizzle | BP 1.4 kHz + 11 Hz tremolo | `water.splash` looped |
| `steel` | plate | — | `metal.hit` |
| `stone` | grind | — | `rubble` |
| `arcane` | choir wash | LP 900 | `cast.magic` |
| `shadow` | breath | LP 600, near-silent | `ped.breath` |
| `light` | shimmer | HP 5 kHz | `fx.forcefield` |
| `air` | pressure | BP sweeping | `swing.air` |
| `shock` | crackle | `_crackle` (`audio.js:334`) | `fx.glitch` |

⚠ **The six pure-synthesis `sustain()` kinds (§0) are exactly the GRAIN beds for ice/toxic/acid/
shadow/light/air.** Building this axis *is* closing the "no coded sound effects for attacks" gap.
They stop being seven hand-written `if (kind === …)` branches (`audio.js:619-700`) and become one

#### AXIS 3 — **BODY**, a frequency. From the ability's own mass and the caster's rank.

```js
const mass = (a.damage || a.dps || 0) + (a.blast || 0) * 1.4 + (a.radius || 0) * 3;
const tim  = Math.pow(2, (40 - Math.min(120, rankOf(def))) / 110);   // heavier fighter = lower voice
const body = (210 * Math.pow(0.72, Math.log2(1 + mass / 8)) + 34) * tim;   // Hz
```

Same role as a firearm's `body`, and `audio.js:841-842` already states why it is the identifying
layer: *"58 Hz reads as a cannon and 165 Hz reads as a machine pistol, at the same loudness."*

#### AXIS 4 — **TAIL**, seconds. How long the world rings.

```js
const tail = 0.06 + Math.min(0.55, (a.range || a.speed || 0) / 900 + (a.blast || 0) / 60);
```

Long-range and high-blast ring; a point-blank jab does not. This is also the axis that makes a
power read differently **indoors** — see §6.4.

### 5.3 ⚠ THE MEASURED RESULT — 212 distinct voices from 364 slots, largest bucket 12

Run against the live roster with the derivation above, quantising each scalar to a just-noticeable
step (crack 1/20, body 1/12-octave, tail 25 ms, grain 0.1):

| | Count |
|---|---|
| slots | 364 |
| **distinct voices** | **212** |
| **largest bucket** | **12** |
| **singleton voices** (a sound belonging to exactly one ability) | **156** |

For comparison, the same measurement without the caster-rank term (Axis 3's `tim`) gives **117

⚠ **The largest buckets are the utility families, and that is correct rather than a defect.**
Without the rank term they are: 61× `field/energy` (the buffs) and 51× `arc/energy` (the evades).
Fifty-two evades *should* be one family — CLAUDE.md's evade system deliberately gives the roster five

bodies differ. **Two abilities with identical numbers cast by identical fighters should sound
identical; the system's job is to guarantee that abilities which differ by the numbers differ by
the ear.** That is the property to assert (§7), not "364 unique sounds".

### 5.4 CHARGE IS THE FIFTH AXIS, AND IT IS ALREADY WIRED

`charge` scales everything else in this engine, and the audio path is already correct in two places:
`abilities.js:307` passes `0.5 + c01 * 1.1` to `kiRelease`, and `bowLoose(t)` passes draw
(`abilities.js:516`). **Extend, do not rebuild:** charge multiplies `mass` before BODY and TAIL are
derived, so a full-charge orb is genuinely lower and rings longer.

⚠ **Two charge releases are constants and should not be**: `growingorb` fires `kiRelease(1.4)`
regardless of size (`abilities.js:322`), and `nova` fires `kiRelease(0.15)` per feed tick
(`abilities.js:789`) while the whole point of `nova` is that the fed amount varies. Both should read
`st.fed` / the orb's own scale.

⚠ **And clamp the fraction at source.** `audio.js` and `abilities.js:294` both carry the scar:
`st.chargeT` is `undefined` on a release with no charge, `undefined / maxCharge` is `NaN`, and
`NaN < 0.12` is **false**, so NaN passed the fizzle guard into damage, orb scale *and audio*.
`c01` is clamped now; any new charge-shaped ability must clamp its own.

### 5.5 THE CASTER IS PART OF THE VOICE — use `def.rank`, not `def.voicePitch`

| Field | Coverage | Distinct values |
|---|---|---|
| `def.voicePitch` | **22 / 52** | 13 |
| `def.rank` | **52 / 52** | **45** (11 → 120) |
| `def.strength` | 52 / 52 | 8 |

`voicePitch` is the obvious choice and the wrong one — it is missing on 30 heroes and would leave
them all on the default. **`rank` is the axis** (`data/scale.js`, CLAUDE.md § THE RANK LADDER), it is

the fighter, the lower their powers speak* with no per-hero authoring.

⚠ `def.body` (`metal`/`stone`/`energy`/`insect`) already carries a full timbre table for landings
(`audio.js:886-891`). **Fold the same table into GRAIN** — a metal fighter's punch should carry the
plate ring their landing already has. One table, two consumers, the `resistOf` law.

### 5.6 WHERE IT LIVES — `data/sfx.js`, `sfxOf(a, def)`, and one override channel

Build it exactly like `data/visual.js`:

- **`data/sfx.js`** — the vocabularies (`SFX_ATTACK`, `SFX_GRAIN`), the derivation functions, and
  `sfxOf(ability, def)` returning `{ attack, grain, crack, body, tail, grainAmt, rate }`.
  Engine-agnostic; zero Three.js; zero imports from `abilities.js`.
- **`validateSfx(roster)`** — every resolved value must be *in* its vocabulary, mirroring
  `validateVis` (`visual.js:341-352`). An unknown value is a typo, not a variation.
- **`audio.power(voice, pos, opts)`** — one new method, shaped like `gunshot(power, pos, voice)`

  function instead of picking a primitive by hand.
- **`def.sfx = {…}` on an ability overrides any field**, and `openjk.md:2168-2178` argues the same
  block should be the home for the per-power camera override too:
  > *"an optional `camera: { range, vert, horz, pitch, damp }` block that a def, an ability row, or a
  > `MODE_IMPL` entry can declare, plus a per-parameter override channel… so a power can take the
  > range and leave the damping alone."*
  **One `fx: { camera: {…}, sfx: {…} }` block, per-parameter override, not all-or-nothing.**

⚠ **This must not become a 45th audio primitive beside the 12 that exist.** The existing methods stay
as the LAYER implementations; `audio.power()` is the *composer* that calls them, the way `gunshot`
composes `sample('gun.crack')` with three generated layers. If `TYPES.melee` still hand-picks
`zap(680)` after this ships, the system did not land.

⚠ **`gunshot()` must keep its own path.** `rifle` already has 13 hand-tuned voices that measure
distinct; running it through the generic derivation would flatten them. `sfxOf` returns `null` for
`a.weapon` and `rifle` keeps calling `gunshot(power, pos, voiceOf(id))` — the same way `visOf` returns
`build`/`temper` only for beams (`visual.js:274-275`).

## 6. THE CLOSE CAMERA — and the thing altitude has to do to the mix

### 6.1 The falloff curve was calibrated for a camera 200 units away, and produces no gradient at 15

`_pg(pos, reach)` (`audio.js:69-74`) is linear:

```js
const d = Math.hypot((pos.x ?? 0) - this._lx, (pos.z ?? 0) - this._lz);
const g = 1.12 - d / reach;
return g <= 0.06 ? 0 : Math.min(1, g);
```

It clips to 1.0 for all `d < 0.12 · reach`. With the shipped reaches:

| Family | reach | Full-gain radius | At a 20u chase engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| `punch.med` | 150 | 18 u | **flat 1.0** — no cue |
| `boom` | 250 | 30 u | **flat 1.0** |
| `ki.blast` | 120 | 14 u | 1.0 → 0.95 |
| `swing.fist` | 80 | 9.6 u | 1.0 → 0.87 |

`world.chase()` clamps the eye 24–86 u from the subject (`world.js:2287`) with `FRAME_MAX = 52`
(`world.js:2285`), and the engagements it frames are 5–50 u. **At every distance the third-person
camera ever shows you, every combat sound is at maximum gain.** The near field has no dynamics at
all: a punch landing on your own face and one landing 15 u away are identical.

**Fix: an inverse-square-with-a-knee curve, and one number to keep the far field where it is.**

```js
_pg(pos, reach = 130) {
  if (!pos || !this._hasL) return 1;
  const d = this._dist(pos);                       // §6.2 — 3-D
  const knee = this.nearField || 3.0;              // metres-ish: 3u ≈ 0.57 m at 1u = 0.19 m
  const g = (knee * knee) / ((knee + d) * (knee + d));
  const far = 1 - Math.min(1, d / reach);          // hard horizon: nothing carries past `reach`
  const v = g * (0.35 + 0.65 * far) * (reach / 130) ** 0.35;
  return v <= 0.02 ? 0 : Math.min(1, v);
}
```

⚠ **The `reach` semantics MUST be preserved.** Thirty-plus call sites and every `MANIFEST` row carry
a tuned `reach` (`samples.js:25-113`), and CLAUDE.md states the design intent as a contract:
*"booms carry ~240u, cracks ~110u, screams ~190u."* Change the curve inside the horizon; do not
change where the horizon is.

⚠ **Calibrate the knee against the CHASE distance, not by ear on the iso camera.** The measurable
(§7) is: the gain ratio between a sound at 5 u and one at 25 u must exceed **6 dB** in chase mode.
It is currently **0 dB**.

### 6.2 ⚠ THE LISTENER IS TWO-DIMENSIONAL, AND POWERWORLD IS NOT

```js
listen(x, z) { this._lx = x; this._lz = z; this._hasL = true; }        // audio.js:55
if (this.player) this.audio.listen(this.player.pos.x, this.player.pos.z);   // game.js:3592
```

**Y is never captured and never compared.** In the city this is nearly harmless — the fight is
within one band of the ground. In PowerWorld it is the defining failure:

- `BANDS.ceiling` is at minimum **260** (`MIN_CEIL`, CLAUDE.md), the flagship's is 328, and
  PowerWorld lifts the cap entirely — measured climbs to **1,476 u** (`world.setSpace` reaches 1.00
  there, CLAUDE.md § POWERWORLD).
- A police cruiser, a car explosion, a pedestrian scream or a district hazard **directly below you at
  1,400 u** is at *zero horizontal distance* and therefore plays at **full gain**.
- Conversely, two fliers 300 u apart *vertically* but stacked in plan hear each other perfectly.

**Fix.** `listen(x, y, z)`, a 3-D distance, and — because altitude is the mode switch —
an explicit vertical weight:

```js
listen(x, y, z) { this._lx = x; this._ly = y ?? 0; this._lz = z; this._hasL = true; }
_dist(pos) {
  const dx = (pos.x ?? 0) - this._lx, dz = (pos.z ?? 0) - this._lz;
  const dy = ((pos.y ?? this._ly) - this._ly) * (this.vertWeight ?? 1);
  return Math.hypot(dx, dy, dz);
}
```

⚠ **`pos.y ?? this._ly` is load-bearing.** Dozens of sites pass `{ x, z }` object literals with no Y
(`game.js:2297, 2330, 2387`, `powerworld.js:410`). Defaulting a missing Y to *the listener's own Y*
keeps every one of those sounding exactly as it does today — the change is additive, and it cannot
regress the city. Verify that: a city duel's measured mix must be unchanged (§7).

⚠ **`vertWeight` is not 1.** In a city, altitude should attenuate *harder* than plan distance — a
street fight below a flier should recede fast (`1.35`). In PowerWorld, where every axis is a fighting
axis, it should be **1.0**. Set it from `f._openSky` at the same place the other four open-sky rules
live (`entity.js`, manual §46) so it is one dimension, not a fifth exception.

### 6.3 THE AIR/GROUND TRANSITION — what the ear does when the mode switches

The thesis says altitude is the mode switch. Four things must move with it, and each already has a
gate to hang on.

| Cue | Gate | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **The city recedes** | `bandOf(y)` (`core/util.js`) | `soundscape` ducks its `traffic`/`crowd` layers toward zero over the GROUND→BUILDING→SKY bands. Already easeable — the bed crossfades over ~1 s (CLAUDE.md § SOUND) |
| **The air thins** | `world.setSpace(0..1)` — *already computed every frame in PowerWorld* | One number, already smootherstepped at both ends, driving one lowpass on the `ambient` bus. `world.setSpace` reaches 1.00 at 1,476 u; **it is the honest, already-derived "air is running out" fraction** and CLAUDE.md's own law for it is *"it must touch NOTHING else"* — a filter on one bus honours that |
| **Airspeed** | `_chaseKb` / cruise | `pw-bfp-source.md:581-587` warns BFP's 8.7× speed produced *"fights nobody can follow."* A wind bed whose intensity is airspeed is the cheapest possible answer, and `sustain('fire')` already models exactly this shape for the afterburner (`f._burnLoop`, CLAUDE.md § manual §20) |
| **Ground contact** | `f.grounded` | `land()` already types the impact by body. Touching down should also *restore* the city bed — the JKA half of the thesis begins with a sound |

⚠ **None of this may run in the city.** Everything above is gated on `f._openSky` /
`body.powerworld`, and the PowerWorld verification law (CLAUDE.md § manual §47) applies verbatim:
*"THE CITY IS UNCHANGED, MEASURED"* — the audio suite must assert a city duel's mix is bit-identical
after a PowerWorld match, by driving the fallbacks rather than reasoning about them.

### 6.4 STEREO IS THE ONE THING WORTH ADDING, AND IT IS NEARLY FREE

Every sound in this engine is mono — `_pg` returns a scalar and everything connects to a bus gain.
At an isometric camera that is defensible. **At a chase camera it is the largest single readability
loss**, because the camera is *behind you* and half of what matters is off-screen.

`StereoPannerNode` costs one node per voice. The pan value is the sound's world position projected
onto the camera's right vector — and `world.chase()` already computes exactly that perpendicular:

```js
const px = az / Math.hypot(ax, az || 1e-6), pz = -ax / Math.hypot(ax, az || 1e-6);   // world.js:2306
```

⚠ **Use the DAMPED camera basis, not the raw one.** `openjk.md:2116-2131` (§7.3) makes the argument
for the camera and it applies identically here: a fast flick that snaps the pan field is a smear.
`camPos`/`camTarget` are already damped (`world.js:2292-2311`) — read those.

⚠ **Pan only in chase mode.** An isometric camera has no meaningful left/right for the *player*, and
panning a top-down view is the classic mistake that makes headphones worse than speakers.

⚠ **Cap the pan at ±0.7.** A hard-panned combat cue in headphones reads as broken, not as directional.

## 7. THE MEASURABLE — `src/bench/audio.js`, and the trap that cost the last one 10 of 13

The armory pass proved 13 firearms distinct through an analyser. That harness was thrown away.
**This one gets committed**, in the idiom of `src/bench/powerworld.js`, exposed as
`await window.LSW.audioSuite()`.

### 7.1 ⚠ THE TRAP, FIRST — measure with `rAF`, never `setTimeout`

CLAUDE.md § THE ARMORY records it, and it is the single reason to write this section before the code:

> *"MEASURE WITH rAF, NOT `setTimeout` — a transient is ~40 ms; the throttled harness caught only
> 3/13 and gave two same-voice weapons different numbers."*

A background/throttled tab clamps `setTimeout` to ≥1 s (and ≥4 ms even foregrounded, nested). An
attack transient is 30–60 ms. A sampler running on `setTimeout` **misses the part of the sound that
carries the identity** and reads the tail, so two identical weapons measure different and two
different ones measure the same. Both failure directions, from one wrong timer.

```js
// the only correct sampler shape in this repo
const grab = (analyser, frames) => new Promise((res) => {
  const out = []; const buf = new Float32Array(analyser.fftSize);
  const step = () => {
    analyser.getFloatTimeDomainData(buf);
    let s = 0; for (let i = 0; i < buf.length; i++) s += buf[i] * buf[i];
    out.push(Math.sqrt(s / buf.length));
    if (out.length < frames) requestAnimationFrame(step); else res(out);
  };
  requestAnimationFrame(step);
});
```

⚠ **And the tab must be foregrounded.** CLAUDE.md's `_ema` artefact (*"a hidden browser pane renders
at 0×0"*) has an audio twin: a backgrounded tab throttles rAF to ~10 Hz, which is 100 ms per sample —
worse than the transient. The suite must **refuse to run** when `document.hidden`, and say so.

### 7.2 What gets measured

Tap the master bus once (`audio.master.connect(analyser)`, `fftSize 2048`, `smoothingTimeConstant 0`)
and take, per sound, over **24 rAF frames ≈ 400 ms**:

| Metric | How | Why it is the right one |
|---|---|---|
| **peak RMS** | max of the frame RMS series | did it make a sound at all |
| **onset frame** | first frame above 3× the noise floor | is the transient where it should be |
| **decay τ** | frames from peak to 10% of peak | this is TAIL, measured |
| **spectral centroid** | Σ(f·mag) / Σ(mag) on `getFloatFrequencyData` at the peak frame | this is CRACK + GRAIN, measured — and it is the number that separated the 13 firearms |
| **low-band ratio** | Σ mag < 200 Hz / Σ mag total | this is BODY, measured |
| **signature** | `[centroid, lowRatio, τ, peak]` as a 4-vector | the thing distinctness is computed on |

### 7.3 The assertions

## 10. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION

The recording ruling and the "generated, no AI" ruling are the same ruling and `gunshot()` already
satisfies both: a CC0 recording for the transient, our own DSP for everything else, thirteen weapons
that measure thirteen apart. **Six `sustain()` kinds covering 17 ability slots are the only genuine
synthesised attack voices left, and the only document actually in breach is
`docs/VOICE_AND_SFX.md`, which still plans AI TTS barks.** The real emergency is not the gap — it is
**D1**: a unit mismatch between `performance.now()` and `ctx.currentTime` means the sustain watchdog
kills every recorded loop within 0.4 seconds, so the beam voice, the charge spool and the
flamethrower roar are all dead right now and the intensity-driven beam strain — which
`POWERWORLD.md:466` calls *"already correct by ESF's standard"* — cannot be heard at all. After
that: a third of the roster's 364 abilities resolve to seven sounds, and the fix is not assets but
the `VOICES` pattern generalised — `ATTACK × GRAIN × BODY × TAIL`, derived from `visOf().shape`,
`visOf().material`, the ability's own damage/blast/radius and the caster's `rank`, which measures
**212 distinct voices with a largest bucket of 12 and 156 singletons, using zero new files.** Then
the camera: the falloff curve is flat across every distance a chase camera ever shows, the listener
has no Y at all in a mode whose entire premise is altitude, and 77 combat calls pass no position, so
none of it would reach them anyway.
