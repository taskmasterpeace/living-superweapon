# Resource construct Task 3 — hit-routing preflight

September 8, 2026. Read-only review of Task 3 in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-08-resource-constructs-brief.md` against the current dirty checkout. No runtime changes, tests or browser runs. Tank construction/movement was not reviewed. Line anchors are inspection-time references; use the named symbols if concurrent edits move them.

## Preserve these native routing boundaries

1. **There are two projectile dispatch paths, plus an early guided hit.** `Projectiles.update` (`src/engine/projectiles.js:1667–1687`) sends ballistic, charged and collision-priority shots through `_projectileContacts`; ordinary nonparticipating shots call `Projectile.update`. Guided `update` (`:278–322`) substeps and calls `_impact` immediately after `sweepSplitObstacle`, before `advancePrepared/commitContact`. Therefore a callback only in `commitContact` misses that early route; a callback added globally to `_impact` cannot reliably identify which surface was struck and will conflate direct and explosion damage. Retain the earliest query's `kind/target/t` at the contact branch, and let native explosive hits reach their one splash route.

2. **Classify damage from actual native behavior, not appearance or nominal blast radius.** The constructor (`projectiles.js:120–125,153–158`) substitutes a nonzero blast when `o.blast` is zero. `bullet` is a visual flag; `_impact` (`:630–662`) uses **`ballistic`** to decide whether to skip `areaDamage`. For ordinary non-ballistic impacts, construct damage belongs exclusively to `Game.areaDamage(damage * 0.8, ...)`; do not also charge `projectile.damage` at cover contact. Guided split children are normally explosive, so their early hit must still cause only splash billing. A ballistic shot can have a positive `blast` field and must still receive one direct debit, not a splash debit.

3. **Notify at the committed surface, before branch-specific movement, without changing that branch.** `Projectile.commitContact` (`:454–502`) handles fuse, ground, boomerang return, arm-delay, box/cylinder ricochet and ordinary impact. Explosive armed/remote payloads spend nothing on first touch; charge only when a real detonation reaches `areaDamage`. A ballistic ricochet bills its real contact once before its position is moved outside the box. Capture the point before that move; do not report the separated post-bounce point. Preserve arm/return/bounce/dispose semantics instead of replacing these branches with unconditional explosion or removal.

   `_projectileContacts` (`:1707–1767`) already supplies an authoritative event and a per-shot ignored-surface set across its substeps; it adds the target after `commitContact` to prevent repeated t=0 hits in that frame. Do not create a permanent shot/construct blacklist, or legitimate later contacts will never bill. Intercepted/neutralized shots (`:1745–1758`) are disposed without `_impact`; they must not create construct splash. Armed/stuck fuse precedence is also explicit in `earliestOrdinaryContact` (`src/engine/attack-interception.js:46–50`).

4. **Finite-bottom bounds need consistent entry and bounce geometry.** `coverBoxEntry` / `sweepSplitObstacle` (`src/engine/projectile-contact.js:3–23,28–69`) currently clip only an upper Y plane; missing `bottom` must keep that legacy behavior. The manager's box path delegates to `coverBoxEntry` (`attack-interception.js:54`), while guided/beam paths use `sweepSplitObstacle`. Extend both through the shared opted-in box helper, with coherent radius/top-padding semantics. Also inspect the box ricochet face selection (`projectiles.js:472–484`): it considers **top only** and always chooses positive Y for vertical reflection. Merely adding a bottom slab can make an upward underside contact eject above the box or reflect in the wrong direction. Keep the correction restricted to records with finite `bottom`. Fist sweeps need radius 0.42 vertically as well as horizontally, with ground disabled.

5. **`blockedCov` is a candidate, not sufficient proof of the final reached receiver.** In `BeamHose.update`, per-segment clipping stores it (`projectiles.js:1319–1328`); `clipReceivers` and the compressed `_packetContact` path can shorten the hose and clear it (`:1334–1345`); clash pin/`clashLen` handling follows (`:1355–1375`). A construct debit must happen only once at the surviving, actually reached contact after those cutoffs, never along the aim ray or an abandoned tail. Store/copy the candidate contact and verify it is still the endpoint/first reached obstruction of the final source-ordered path; compare path position/arc, not distance from the caster. Do not move a farther candidate forward to the new tip and bill it there.

   `clipForContacts` (`:1182–1193`) is called by the manager before clash/interception and must remain a **side-effect-free clipping pass**. `_clipStreamSegment` (`:1601–1609`) adds a two-ULP spatial skin when re-querying Float32 stored paths; preserve that tolerance or contact callbacks will flicker. Bill only the beam's real sustained update: `dps * caster.powerBuff * dt`. No debit before tip arrival, on a pending launch, after sustain ends or during the released tail. Resource callback must precede the ordinary `blockedCov.hp > 0` branch (`:1378`), which directly subtracts `dps * 2 * dt` and never calls `Game.damageBlock`. Leave that legacy city-cover multiplier intact.

6. **Splash and city damage are separate native lanes.** `Game.areaDamage` (`src/engine/game.js:2274–2295`) applies Fighter damage and then invokes `worldImpact`. Add a separate construct receiver loop to area damage, using the plan's closest-point-to-3D-box falloff and one application of `caster.powerBuff`; the Fighter loop's center/radius formula is not the construct formula. `worldImpact` (`:2315–2329`) can independently call `damageBlock(power * 20 * falloff)`. Explicitly exclude construct records there or a callback will turn one explosion into two energy charges. Dispatch an explicit `damageBlock` construct callback before its missing-HP return (`:2363–2371`), then return without cracks/vents/city shatter. `shatterBlock` (`:2448`) needs the construct safety exclusion before its normal property handling.

   Do not suppress legitimate ground craters or destruction of a real adjacent city block. “No construct city destruction” means the proxy itself does not become property, not that its explosion disables normal surrounding scenery damage. Preserve friendly direct/beam/melee **obstruction with zero debit**, self exclusion, and the plan's half-rate friendly splash. A zero accepted energy charge is not permission for the shot/fist to pass through its physical surface.

7. **Melee must choose one earliest contact before entering any humanoid resolver.** `MeleeSystem.beginContactFrame/endContactFrame` (`src/engine/melee.js:83–101`) snapshot before physics and defer resolution until both final poses exist. `_resolveContact` (`:441–491`) uses the active interval, `m.previous/current`, radius .42 and a minimum segment parameter over torso/head/pelvis; it resolves the last active segment before advancing recovery. Add the resource box candidate to this competition, not a second cone or post-Fighter hit. Use the same segment and the nearest obstacle query so an earlier city cover/interior vetoes a new construct candidate; do not broaden ordinary city-cover melee behavior. Compare authored reach to the actual contact point, not the center of a large wall. Friendly/self construct contact still wins physically and consumes this swing's hit opportunity even when debit is zero.

   Store the **construct object** in `strikeHit` before its callback can retire it; do not pass it to `_resolveLight/_resolveHeavy` (`:251,291`), which expect Fighter IDs, guard state, counter/punish, status fields and `takeDamage`. Preserve the plan's unblocked object formula, attacker hitstop/contact sound and combo/recovery timing. Tests copied from `moving-melee.test.mjs` that replace `_resolveLight` with a spy prove ordering only, not actual damage, costs or recovery.

8. **Receiver callbacks may synchronously remove their own and sibling proxies.** The current `debitConstructKi/exhaustConstructOwner` contract in `src/engine/construct-policy.js:25,70` can retire all resource constructs for a shared owner. A direct/beam callback may therefore invalidate the cover list being traversed; an area-damage callback may remove multiple remaining receivers. Use stable candidate identities/snapshot iteration and recheck each receiver's live eligibility before calling. Capture contact coordinates before disposal and avoid continuing through city mutation for the removed record. Separate later explosions/ricochets remain separate events; do not deduplicate globally across them.
