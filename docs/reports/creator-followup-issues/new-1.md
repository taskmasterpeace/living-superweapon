User concept: wizard duplicates look identical, attack the same target with real damage, and allow deliberate body switching or takeover when the controlled body dies. Audit existing summons/mindcontrol before adding an owner/controller abstraction. Existing magic-tagged roster is MYSTWARD and MARSHAL; no complete duplicate-possession system was confirmed.

Proposed bounded first trial: original + 2 copies, one shared energy pool and action cooldown reservation, reduced per-copy damage so duplicate count cannot multiply damage without limit. Copies consume shared aim/action intent but use their own reachable movement, line of sight, windup and collision; never teleport shots from the original. Switching body transfers human input/camera, not squad ownership or enemies' knowledge. Surviving eligible copy can take control after KO; all bodies down ends the ability, with normal mission reserve rules afterward.

Counterplay: copies can be interrupted, hit and killed; short switch cooldown/telegraph, visible remaining-body count and one unambiguous controlled-body marker for the player. No invulnerable body-switch loop, recursive duplication or gadget/gear duplication. Scope whether copies are temporary or persistent before implementation; these numbers are a starting proposal.

Acceptance: keyboard/pad/touch switching, blocked shot, moving target, simultaneous KO, cancellation, portal travel and Threat Room reset. Attribute every hit to the proper body and common controller. Register power/AI/animation/VFX/audio recipe and explain it in the threat scanner. Deferred behind #6 and native combat stability.
