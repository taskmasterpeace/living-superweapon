# TRENCH: hold release availability and redesign as a water specialist

Creator playtest feedback, September 11, 2026:
- Maelstrom feels like a duplicate spirit bomb rather than water control.
- Trident melee looks like waving a magic wand and lacks convincing contact.
- Creator requests temporarily unavailable status with identity retained, powers hidden/unknown in public selection, and no deletion of authoring data.

Requested work:
- Exclude TRENCH from release selection and random AI spawning while held; keep the card/name/portrait with an unavailable label. Preserve existing saves and Studio access.
- Explore a distinct water-specialist kit rather than another beam/cone/charged-orb reskin. Present the revised kit before implementing it.
- Fix weapon grip, two-handed trident action, anticipation/contact/recovery and airborne pose compatibility.
- Explain each retained power's range, damage type, resource cost, effect and counterplay.

Acceptance:
- Public unavailable card exposes no active power controls; legacy loadout references resolve safely with an explicit message.
- Revised abilities prove different tactical purposes using native controls against unguarded, guarding, resistant and obstructed targets.
- Actual gameplay demonstrates effects, interruption, depletion, KO and cleanup. A registry entry or animation still alone is not acceptance.

Scope: player-reported issues, not independently reproduced root causes. This issue does not claim a rework is already implemented.
