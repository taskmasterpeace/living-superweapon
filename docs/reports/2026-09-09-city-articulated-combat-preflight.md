# City articulated combat — bounded native ranged preflight

Planning/read-only recommendation for parent approval. No source/test/browser changes or new content. This is separate from the frozen main-city camera review and its HUD/shared-frame/body acceptance findings.

## Outcome

The production Fighter already calls the ranged pose pipeline in every world. Internal `_openSky` gates, not missing city update calls, prevent the physical support and launch-readiness path from running in city encounters. The smallest complete next slice is **native ranged articulation plus final-emitter preparation**, shared by city humans, city bots, two-player actors, PowerWorld and Studio. It is not a cosmetic beam rewrite or an all-animation migration.

Proposed boundary: a named anatomical capability equivalent to `!!fighter.parts?.rig`, e.g. `supportsArticulatedCombat(fighter)` at the existing `hero-rig.js` seam. The exact helper name is an engineering proposal, not a creator ruling. Existing rig construction supplies the named head/torso/arm/hand contract; an imported skin attached through that native rig is not a different game mode. Keep the existing no-rig fallback for low-level/environmental streams.

Do **not** derive this capability from `combatLookActive`, `world._bfpCameraActive`, `isHuman`, `modeId`, camera visibility, current local player, or a persisted fake `_openSky` flag. The same body, source and intent must pay and launch identically when driven by a bot, assigned to P2, unviewed, or rehearsed in Studio. Active presentation ownership still controls the camera/HUD only. Pause does not make an unresolved projectile become a different class of shot.

## Native sequencing and lifecycle invariants

- Preserve Fighter's existing positive simulation step and pose restoration/application order (`entity.js:2250–2255`, `2433–2438`), then the native projectile manager's preparation/payment/update order. No full `Fighter.update` inserted into another full update; no second animation or projectile tick.
- A constructor-time socket is the preceding pose. Pending preparation samples the **final current rig** before commitment. Hand path checks require shoulder/forearm/hand geometry and an open casting palm, not only a corrected wrist quaternion (`projectiles.js:1094–1114`). Head/chest axes remain physically bounded while independently aimed.
- Keep authored source identity and existing slot state authoritative. The capability is neither a slot grant nor permission to bypass unlocks, guard/action busy gates, energy denial, source costs, cooldowns, preparation cancellation or source-specific emitter metadata. An invalid/unsupported definition must not gain a fabricated pose just because the Fighter has a rig.
- Preserve existing source entry payment. Extending real preparation to city changes *when a valid shot can first emit*, not its price; this latency extension must be visible and measured. Never charge preparation sustain, refund/recharge entry every frame, or allow a second channel to launch after an earlier payment exhausted the same pool.
- Before commitment, guard, release/focus cancellation, native incapacitation, KO/removal, slot/source replacement or rig/form rebind must follow the native cancellation contract. No delayed emission from the old socket after resume/reform; an unresolved shot cannot “fall back” into immediate legacy emission when capability disappears. Start with the existing cancellation seams; add only the missing tag/source/rig ownership safeguard proven by a native RED, not a generic epoch framework.
- After commitment, preserve the existing traveled-packet policy: an owner transition may stop new sustain, but must not manufacture or duplicate damage by deleting/re-firing old energy. Native cover, body/shield interception, deflection, direct/splash rules and legal credit stay in their current pipeline.
- Actual native `energyInfinite` keeps its existing funding semantics; it does not make anatomical readiness true. No camera state or renderer presence may alter damage, emission age, cost, cancellation or committed packet count.

## Explicitly outside this first slice

**Melee is not merely a pose flag.** `melee-pose.js:16–18` gates guard, clinch and the path that calls `animateAuthoredStrike`. But `MeleeSystem` only creates `_meleeMotion` under `_openSky && parts.rig` (`melee.js:135–139`), adds a lunge velocity impulse when that motion exists (`144–152`), and switches legacy city active-hit resolution to swept contact (`541`). Simply opening the visual gate cannot supply that missing motion record; simply opening both gates changes native city combat physics/contact. This needs a separate measured contact/lunge decision and regression set. Do not silently change city melee balance in the ranged slice.

The actual source sampler `strike-motion.js:14–28` is not mode-gated: it chooses source-bank clips and model `strikes`/`heavyStrikes` preferences, mirrors to the committed side and deliberately retains procedural armed-heavy fallback. `authored-pose.js:42` is a visual-only retarget/apply utility, not the missing city dispatch. `guard-pose.js:7` also explicitly excludes non-open-sky actors; that is a separate native guard-pose delivery gate, not permission to alter guard legality or soak.

**Source locomotion/jump is separately held.** `ground-motion.js:31,66` and `jump-motion.js:28` exclude city source gait/transition/jump playback. `flight-pose.js` also retains world-specific visual travel limits. These are not prerequisites for the first complete ranged-emitter slice and must not be casually replaced to claim the entire imported movement portfolio works in city. All `_openSky` flight-tier, takeoff, gravity/hover, ceiling, acceleration, speed-cap, guard-regeneration and movement branches in Fighter remain unchanged.

No cosmetic beam redesign, new attack source, animation/media import, generic capability registry, save migration, providers, map design, new physical flight, melee rebalance, source-number edits or runtime implementation is authorized by this report. The report records a bounded implementation recommendation, not a passing result.
