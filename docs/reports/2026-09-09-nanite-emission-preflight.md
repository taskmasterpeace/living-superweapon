## Outcome and parent clarification

The existing charge pipeline is reusable, but a tagged cannon needs explicit pre-payment readiness and a **terminal** final-launch validity gate. A forearm module cannot borrow the firearm wrist correction or the beam's wait-until-ready behavior. The main hazards are paid Guard-transition cleanup, stale/repaired capabilities authorizing an old shot, semantic side mapping, and final transforms after hit reaction/body separation.

Parent clarified during this preflight, and corrected the brief: for a clear launch, native `launchOrigin` remains the finite sphere **center**, `current aperture + launch direction * full physical radius`. Verify the aperture separately against the final live forearm socket. Do not move the sphere onto the socket, shrink its physical damage radius, or change legacy charge cover clipping. This resolves the original Task 2 assertion's aperture-versus-center ambiguity (`power-emission.js:27`, `projectiles.js:326`; `tools/charged-emission.test.mjs:33–55`).

## Native ordering/lifetime hazards

### 1. Guard input masks a new action, but releases an existing paid player/pad charge

- `game.js:67 feedSlot` masks pressed/held when busy, but forwards released; `shift` is its existing exception. Native player/pad call Guard before computing busy (`:3562–3593`, `:3628–3632`). `TYPES.charge` releases on `!inp.held`, even with no explicit release edge (`abilities.js:364–407`). `MeleeSystem.guard` does not prohibit a ranged charging slot (`melee.js:337–346`).
- Therefore an assembled, legal paid cannon charge may have its ordinary one-shot release/fizzle on the Guard-transition frame. Do not gate that release simply on `fighter.guarding`, or let an opposite-arm raised shield create a false hand conflict. Both modules equipped is not sustained guard-and-fire permission. New cannon/toggle presses remain masked; new charge requires a fresh press after Guard lowers.
- Bots are intentionally different: ordinary slot dispatch is skipped entirely while busy (`game.js:3831–3838`), with a separate forced-beam release branch. Do not “fix parity” by globally forwarding bot charge input while guarding. Native `controlPlayer`/`controlPad`/`controlBot` fixtures are necessary; direct `runSlot` under Guard bypasses the gameplay mask.
- Guard's native carry/hanging/status restrictions and `chargingKi` remain distinct from `st.charging` (`melee.js:339`, `entity.js:962–979,1504–1528`). A visible/deployed shield alone is not guard eligibility. Task 2 must not grant physical shield protection prematurely; that is Task 3's publication gate.

### 2. Place structural denial before payment/use stamps; keep the native paid outcomes

- Entry debit and charge-loop creation occur in `beginPaidCharge` (`abilities.js:30–38`); native infinite core records equivalent invested energy but refundable entry cost 0. Held drain, charge ratio and radius are native (`:367–376`). Release below 0.12 refunds **only** the entry fee and sets 0.2s cooldown; successful release uses native cooldown modifiers (`:388–407`). A structural interruption uses `cancelHeldSlot`, spends what was invested, and preserves the maximum existing cooldown (`:73–92`). These outcomes must not be conflated.
- `runSlot`'s source-specific denial must precede affordability feedback and `_slotUse/_lastSlot` stamping (`:1202–1206`) as well as the handler/payment. Do not label assembling/reforming/occupied/obstructed as no-ki. Preserve noPowers/unlock/hanging/disarm gates and `_handsRetry` semantics (`:1143–1200`). If an existing charge becomes invalid, cancel it explicitly; returning early while leaving `st.charging`, orb/gather or loop live would strand it.
- `cancelHeldSlot` currently handles preparations and pending beams, **not a released ordinary Projectile**. A cannon's released shot is not `st.active` (except a possible remoteShot reference for a remote source). A critical break between release and final resolution therefore needs cancellation of that slot's unresolved cannon shot too. Scope by caster + slot + epoch, never cancel the other arm or already-traveling energy.

### 3. Resolve once at the final native boundary; rejection must never wait for repair

- `Projectile` captures `_powerOrigin` and a cloned command target at construction (`projectiles.js:105–118`); `resolveLaunch` currently sets `_launchResolved`, reacquires a generic origin, applies cover clipping, then flashes (`:326–360`). There is no ordinary Projectile caster-alive/capability gate. A direct read-only probe confirmed this path still resolves a pending private tagged charge after KO; this is a missing **Task 2 integration**, not a request to change legacy shots.
- Game resolves launches before portals (`game.js:3017–3020`), not only in `Projectiles.update` (`projectiles.js:1695–1697`). The same method is also called by `prepareMotion` (`:368`). Put capability/axis/obstruction rejection in this shared final resolver and ensure a rejected object is dead/removed via native `_dispose` **before** any flash, portal hop, prepared collision, motion or damage. A silent `return` leaving it unresolved/live would let it fire after a later repair or pose correction. Ensure direct native `shot.update` and manager paths both stop after disposal.
- Do not reuse `BeamHose.pendingLaunch` semantics: that path intentionally waits for pose alignment, uses `dt>0` for commitment, and refuses Guard (`:1049,1075–1104,1207–1214`). The cannon contract instead retires invalid release attempts; it also must preserve the paid Guard-transition release.
- Final launch is committed once. Moving/form-changing the caster afterward cannot drag a shot back from its portal destination or retarget it. Already-traveling projectiles retain native ownership after break/KO/removal.

#### Concrete small interruption handoff (proposed, no implementation)

Keep ownership in the existing `Projectiles` manager beside `spawnProjectile`/`resolveLaunches` (`projectiles.js:1690–1697`). Add one focused method, `retirePendingNaniteShots(caster, slot = null)`, where null means all of this caster's unresolved cannon shots. It scans `this.list` without splicing while other native managers may be iterating. Match only ordinary Projectiles with `!dead`, `!_launchResolved`, the exact `caster`, and a source-bound cannon marker plus captured slot/epoch in `_powerOrigin`; when slot is supplied, require that slot too. The proposed transient origin shape is `{naniteForm:'cannon', slot, epoch, ...nativePowerOriginFields}`, populated only from the genuine module, not an imported origin override. No change to package data or generic charge source semantics.

For each match call its existing idempotent `_dispose(game)` (`projectiles.js:706–713`), which removes the scene object, frees owned materials and returns the pooled light. Do **not** call `_impact`, detonate, resolveLaunch or manufacture a flash. The normal manager removes dead entries. Retirement is permanent because `dead` stays true; leaving `_launchResolved=false` cannot permit later direct `resolveLaunch`/`update` revival, so those entry points must honor dead tagged shots too. Do not require the captured epoch to equal the module's *current* epoch when canceling: retirement/new life may already have incremented it. Epoch equality is required to authorize a launch, not to dispose an old pending shot.

Call order/owners:

1. Extend `cancelHeldSlot(c,key)` to ask the manager to retire this caster/key's pending tagged cannon shots **before its current missing-slot early return** (`abilities.js:74`), then retain its ordinary preparation/orb/gather/loop/cooldown cleanup. Non-nanite slots/shots see no new behavior.
2. Task 3's native damage chokepoint performs `damageNanite(...)`, then immediately calls `cancelHeldSlot(owner,result.disabledSlot)` before later frame work can repair/rebind/resolve a shot. Break itself keeps its existing epoch semantics. This also works when release has already cleared `st.charging`.
3. `clearSlotFx` can retire all this caster's pending tagged cannon shots once before its slot loop, covering KO/dispose without depending on surviving slot keys. The source-removal/change branch at the top of `Fighter.update` cancels that key before/alongside `retireNanites`; its cancellation must also work when the slot was deleted. A direct final resolver still validates current source/liveness if no full Fighter.update ran (Studio/direct fixture).
4. `applyForm` is **not** retirement: no pending-shot cancellation there for an equivalent surviving capability; reacquire its new view/socket at final resolution. Final axis/obstruction/source checks remain a second, terminal defense, never a delayed alignment queue.

Preserve native temporal commitment: final melee resolves before `updatePortals/resolveLaunches`, so a melee break/KO there can cancel an unresolved release. Ordinary incoming projectile contacts run after the manager's final-launch resolution; a break arriving then must not retroactively erase already-emitted energy. Test both sides of this boundary rather than broadly moving all incoming damage before launch. This is a tag-scoped interruption seam, not a generic projectile-lifecycle rewrite.

### 4. Capability epoch and view lifetime are different from rig identity

- Current private state stores `slot`, `epoch`, canonical `attackIdentity(source)` and readiness (`nanite-state.js:8–19`); retirement/new life change epoch (`:52–65`). `damageNanite` breaks a cell and returns `disabledSlot`, but does **not** change epoch (`:42–49`). A repaired module has the same capability epoch. Consequently a bare “epoch matches and ready now” check cannot revive a previously invalidated shot; break cancellation must be terminal.
- `Fighter.update` retires removed/changed/invalid sources before presentation using canonical identity (`entity.js:1159–1164`). Controllers execute before that update, and Studio does not run full Fighter.update. Validate the current slot/source in the emitter/readiness resolver as well; do not rely solely on the next live update detecting replacement. Preserve equivalent source reconstruction independent of object-key order; Task 1 now uses `attackIdentity`, not JSON insertion order.
- `applyForm` rebuilds real views and retires their resources while retaining reducer/integrity/epoch (`entity.js:538–613`). `presentNanites` binds each fresh view's epoch (`nanite-forearms.js:107–114`); new sockets are distinct Object3Ds. Pending payloads carry slot/epoch, never an old socket/arm/root. Reacquire from current `parts.nanites`, verify current source, module readiness, matching view epoch and owning Fighter lifecycle. No fallback to palm, another gun or an invisible retired rig.
- KO/dispose retire immediately (`entity.js:646,1101`); respawn begins new assembly (`:1569`). Partial hitstop frames skip state advancement (`:1534–1536`). Presentation or emitter queries cannot advance assembly/repair, including Studio pose settling. Task 1's views expose `socket/root/arm/fit/layout`, but their presence/visibility alone is not readiness or an implemented attack.

### 5. Anatomical side must come from attachment, and final aim must move the forearm

- Right-forearm is legacy `parts.armL.children[1]`, left-forearm is `parts.armR.children[1]` (`nanite-forearms.js:73`; `hand-emission.js:1–9`). Hand is sibling child 2, **not** a descendant of the forearm. `bendArm` synchronizes both; `reachArm` expects a target in the arm parent's space and a matching elbow pole (`hero-rig.js:46–78`). Local cannon forward is −Y.
- Current `castHandMask` only interprets generic castHand/style for charge (`cast-channels.js:6–13`). Task 1's raw private right-forearm fixture has no castHand; the probe gets mask **2** while its actual view is **armL**, which must reserve mask **1**. `materializeAttack` derives correct castHand for authored definitions (`attack-tuning.js:51–55`), but raw trusted native definitions also need correct tagged routing. Derive the cannon mask from attachment; do not rename old rig/socket/volley conventions.
- `firearmEmitter` searches visible weapon descendants of the fist and has deliberate generic fallbacks (`weapon-emission.js:3–19`). `combat-pose.js:432–465` re-aims the **hand quaternion** and retracts grip-mounted weapons. The read-only probe rotates the sibling wrist by 1 rad and measures **0 rad** cannon-axis change. A correct projectile ray cannot establish actual barrel aim.
- Use the bounded parent-forearm adapter and current fitted socket offset, not wrist-only correction or bone reparenting. Task 1 cancels the fore mesh's nonuniform scale at the attachment root (`nanite-forearms.js:76–79`): do not apply body scale twice. Recompute direction from the actual aperture to the command target through up to three feasible solves, then measure the real final aperture axis. Behind/too-close targets and unreachable torso-safe poses must deny rather than silently launch an independent ray.

### 6. Final pose ownership, recoil and recovery must remain coherent

- Current order is body/locomotion → `animateCombatAim`/hands (`entity.js:2420–2426`) → measured offensive fist speed (`:2585–2600`) → `animateHitReaction` → limb surfaces/skin → `presentNanites` → `syncChargePresentation` (`:2603–2610`). Add the bounded forearm adapter after the hit-reaction carrier and before skin/presentation/charge sync. Do not measure its presentation correction as extra offensive fist speed.
- The release handler has already set `st.charging=false`; it marks ranged pose recovery via `_poseWritten/_poseUntil` (`abilities.js:386–394,1212–1214`). An adapter that only runs for `st.charging` will lose the release frame. It must account for its unresolved release/recovery while respecting native hard action ownership, not keep the arm permanently aimed because the attachment exists.
- `restoreCombatBase` samples/restores prior combat overlays (`combat-pose.js:71–86`), while `restoreHitReaction` restores both forearm and fist only when its own carrier was applied (`hit-reaction.js:42–52`). A new final correction must unwind deterministically on next pose/form handoff even when there was **no** hit-reaction snapshot. Test repeated `_animate(0)`, interruption and lowering Guard for no accumulated transforms or bind reset; do not change the generic firearm correction to hide a nanite-specific issue.
- `Game.resolveBodies` can translate a Fighter after `_animate`, then re-syncs charge presentation (`game.js:1652–1678`); portals re-sync held fields again (`:3052`). Sampling final matrices must include those changes. A moved root can change close-target parallax after the arm solve, so final emitter validation must recheck axis/envelope, not trust an earlier cached boolean. Do not broadly rearrange Game.update.

### 7. Radius fitting, obstruction and feedback have separate ownership

- `syncChargePresentation` currently iterates slot values and builds generic `_powerOrigin` fields (`power-emission.js:42–60`). Tagged origin identity must survive that loop, `fitChargeRadius`'s repeated radius changes, gather placement and release payload. A failed tagged lookup must propagate a denial/cancellation, not leave stale vectors, NaN, or generic muzzle fallback.
- Visual core fitting tests the expanded radius against terrain/cover/interiors and may compress **presentation** (`power-emission.js:11–21`). Physical release radius/damage stay native. `Projectile.resolveLaunch` also sweeps aperture→full center (`projectiles.js:341–347`). A legal barrel with an expanded sphere meeting a nearby wall should retain the existing native clipping/contact behavior. A barrel/aperture itself intersecting cover/body or an axis error >3° is the distinct nanite terminal denial.
- `constrainWeaponCover` is a fist/held-weapon bounds adapter with its own cache and recovery (`weapon-cover.js:8–52`); it is not a forearm-envelope proof. Reuse the native obstacle queries, including interiors, finite top/bottom and terrain (`projectile-contact.js:22–69`), while testing actual fitted barrel/plate and torso/head volumes. The legacy helper skips certain `t<=0` corner contacts; do not copy that as permission for a cannon aperture starting inside a wall.
- Existing charge release sound, camera punch and shake happen immediately in the handler (`abilities.js:407`), whereas the launch flash is already deferred. The brief explicitly forbids flash/damage after final denial but does not choose the exact release-audio/punch behavior for a shot rejected only after pose. Record that narrow feedback choice with parent before claiming all launch feedback is final-gated; no new audio implementation is required or authorized here.
