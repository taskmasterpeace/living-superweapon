## Scope and native paths

Actual intact cell unit boxes, their fitted world transforms and cached physical body volumes supply contact geometry. Cuff/saddle/vents/fragments are not armor. Native ballistic/charged and plain ordinary projectiles, simple native guided children, already-traveled non-piercing hoses and active-window fists carry one selected owning-Fighter contact into the existing damage receiver. There are no fake Fighters, cover entries, fog boxes, city hit receivers or reducer-only browser hits.

A bounded lookahead derived from the receiver's current cell envelope may defer an overly generous body proxy. It never creates a future contact, damage or beam endpoint; each finite subsegment must actually arrive, and each query checks current source/view/epoch/deployment/cell integrity. Outboard cells need not intersect the generic body proxy; physical torso/head/pelvis entry still wins over a cell behind it. Earlier native world/other-target winners retain their existing ordering.

Contact capabilities are one-use, privately branded objects. Copied/plain metadata cannot authorize damage. Source replacement, retraction, form/view replacement and a broken cell invalidate a queued token; a retained old token cannot bill a repaired cell or DoT. A full panel soak reports zero HP through the native callback while preserving one guard-meter/push/hitstop/blocked-strike consequence. Local integrity and absorbed damage are separate from HP, XP, combo and damage statistics. Body armor/shieldpack do not consume a full panel soak; residual damage uses their original pipeline once.

Native projectile reflection remains authoritative for cannon/ineligible shields and when zero resistance, native ballistic plate/toughness, phase or invulnerability prevent positive accepted absorption. `damage-admission.js` extracts only the canonical pre-armor numeric stages. The existing `takeDamage` branches still commit mood-critical consumption, downed/wake behavior, air-superiority launch, plate cues/zero callbacks, immunity, magic siphon and acid effects at their original points. A real eligible projectile panel can preview those numbers, then a private synchronous options-object WeakMap supplies that same result to the single receiver call. It expires on consumption and in `finally`; there is no public numeric override, saved admission or second formula.

Beam-held cell tokens are revalidated before geometry restoration, including same-epoch break. Removing a panel releases the old cap and requires newly traveled continuation. Beam integrity billing runs each accepted dt; existing bounded contact presentation is used rather than spawning full metal bursts every frame. Discrete contacts use actual metal surface points/normals. RED-proven full-soak and residual positive shield absorption suppress only the duplicate generic body-center flash and guard-sphere ripple. Native HP, kill credit, guard meter/push/hitstop, blocked callbacks and existing blocked XP/hitFlash policy remain unchanged. No broad `contactFx` flag is added for residual damage. Caller-supplied results are cleared by the receiver; untagged, cannon and ineligible zero-absorption contacts keep native body feedback.

Critical cannon breaks call the existing own-slot cancellation seam. Final melee can retire unresolved energy before the native launch prepass. An incoming projectile after that prepass cannot erase already launched energy; the other arm's charge survives. Repair never revives cancelled preparation.

Splash, DoT, chain/fuse payloads, grabs/clinch throws and guard-crush bypasses do not inherit a cell token. These are remaining full-brief/future coverage boundaries, **not** a claim that all attack variants or arbitrary custom meshes are supported.
