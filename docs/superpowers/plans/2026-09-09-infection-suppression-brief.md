## Chosen slice and rejected shortcuts

Choose **separate bounded native gates**: source-selective inhibitor contact → explicit alien allies/enemies → same-person human infection → finite-range spitter → bounded human-Fighter horde → ambient civilian handoff and crowd-scale proof. No catalog/source/encounter entry is exposed before its entire playable gate works.

Rejected shortcuts: `noPowers`/stun for suppression; origin-regex hostility/eligibility; a poison DoT relabeled infection; minion particles or hero survival waves called zombies; ballistic flags to avoid spit explosions; an unbounded generic status/ECS/faction rewrite. A fully instanced combat-crowd engine may eventually be justified by measurements, but is not assumed before a bounded native prototype.

The first human-Fighter gate is useful but **does not complete the user's human infection/horde request**. Ambient civilian conversion and crowd-scale acceptance remain required, explicit open work. The large crowds cannot be claimed complete by increasing the cap without measurement.

## Proposed contracts requiring approval

### 1. Source-selective active-power suppression

First effect: a visible, traveling **inhibitor dart**, not a citywide field or compulsory detention mechanic. Prototype victims explicitly opt into suppression of a small trusted set of their active attack sources. Initial supported cancellation adapters: ordinary `projectile`, `charge`, and non-remote sustained `beam`; equipment is never included. Reject unsupported victim-source policies at setup instead of pretending every power type has a safe interruption implementation.

- Resolve each registered victim slot using its genuine `attackSource(def, slot)` and `attackIdentity(source)`. Store `{slot, identity, kind:'power'|'equipment'}` in encounter-owned state after comparing the genuine source to the private approved source table. Read trusted `_gearHeld.base`/catalog entry for pickup classification. Unknown source, forged match or unsupported form refuses the opt-in setup; do not fall back to “non-gear means power.”
- No new persisted classification on existing roster or source snapshots. Imported presentation/attack overrides cannot change power into equipment or vice versa. Match the original source after tuning, not the effective tuned attack. A source replacement invalidates the old binding immediately; cosmetic form changes preserve classification only while the genuine source matches.
- Suppression prevents **new use/control and preparation/sustain** for those registered active sources. It does not reduce max ki, drain/refund ki, pause cooldowns, rewrite strength/resistance, disable physical melee/guard, remove equipment/gadgets, cancel flight or change F/SPACE/descend/evade controls. Passive powers, barrier/phase physiology, transformations, summons, mines, constructs, nanite assemblies and non-slot movement remain outside this first effect. Name/document it as an *active-attack inhibitor*, not total anti-power containment.
- Infinite-core energy remains pinned by the native helper. Infinite energy is not automatically immunity to this non-energy source permission, but a target must still be explicitly opted in and the dart must pass its native defenses. Do not erase a powerful target's resistances to make a demo work.
- Apply only on a valid live target's positive, unblocked HP-damage result from the authorized dart; invulnerability, phase, resistance immunity, fully absorbing armor/shield and frontal guard prevent suppression. Blocking that still chips hp does not apply it. Deflection transfers attack credit through the native caster policy, but can only suppress another explicitly registered eligible source set.
- On application, synchronously cancel affected preparations without release/refund; stop emitting sustained beams while already-traveled packets finish normally; preserve launched projectile damage/ownership. Permanently retire affected **unresolved** source-tagged shots, even if suppression later expires. The interruption path is scoped to registered source/life tags and never changes untagged legacy charge behavior. A read-only launch-time check alone is insufficient because an unresolved shot could fire after expiry.
- Repeated blocked inputs cannot queue a surprise attack. Keep a denied-held latch until a physical release and fresh press; AI clears/reselects suppressed actions rather than repeatedly requesting a dead slot. Remote detonation, split shots and other committed control sources are unsupported in the first victim-source policy; do not silently strip the remote branch from every slot.
- Proposed timing: 2.5s active suppression, **no refresh while active**, then 2s target recovery immunity to this effect. This avoids a crowd maintaining permanent denial. Another accepted dart can still do its ordinary contact damage; no nested multiplier/stacks. Proposed dart: 2 physical damage, speed 70u/s, total center-path budget 55u, radius 0.35u, no splash/knockback/launch, source cost 4 ki, cooldown 2s. These are new-source settings, not changed legacy balance.
- Visible contract: small warm-amber suppression marker/countdown on the victim and a specific source-denial explanation on affected slots. No purple, giant screen wash, faked DRAINED event or repeated sound spam. Marker lifetime follows effect state and clears with it.

Full power suppression, fields/cuffs, resistance-strength competition, grounding airborne targets, passive-power negation and construct/nanite consequences remain **design holds**, not implied by this gate.

### 3. Living human host → infected human, without a roster rewrite

Eligibility is explicit runtime enrollment: `hostKind:'human'`, encounter-owned, alive, local non-player, unarmed procedural human Fighter, no respawn. No `deriveOrigin`/name/body-color heuristic. Existing player heroes, police, aliens, robots, energy bodies, dummies, remote actors, minions, constructs, nanites and ambient pedestrian indices are **not enrolled by default**. A later broader human-hero policy requires approval; being human is not permission to override a user's character/control/save.

Proposed first transmission: **infectious unarmed light hand contact**, not an invented bite animation. Route through the native animated fist → target rig → `takeDamage` → `onHit` chain. Eligible infected light attacks carry an unforgeable encounter source/action tag into damage options; heavy/grab/slam, thorns, DoT, blast and arbitrary nearby actors do not inherit infection. One valid contact/action can dose one target only once. Use the actual `blocked` result, not the attacker-side guard prediction. Spit adds the same exposure only after its own accepted direct contact gate.

Proposed state machine: `susceptible → exposed → incubating → infected → dead/removed`. Exposure doses are 50 of a 100 threshold; once threshold is reached incubation is 3s, no refresh/acceleration by further contacts. Sub-threshold exposure does not decay in the first short encounter. It clears on encounter reset/removal/KO. No resurrection or posthumous conversion; KO wins a same-frame conversion tie. Cure/recovery items, infection immunity windows and persistence outside the encounter remain open proposals, not implemented research rewards.

Conversion preserves the **same Fighter object, runtime id, pos/vel, hp/maxHp, wounds, current native statuses and existing damage attribution**. It does not restore health, call `_ko`, respawn, add a duplicate actor, change a roster def, or synthesize XP/kill/civilian-death events. Deferred commit changes only the encounter state, team and controlled NPC intent role, then clears action preparation/grab/input ownership. An infected NPC's subsequent attack is credited to that NPC, not indefinitely to the original infector. Keep the original infector runtime id as bounded informational provenance only.

Proposed human hosts: hp 60, speed field 14, strength 2, flightTier 0, no equipment/items/slots except a private spitter source for an explicitly authored spitter role. No strength/damage/health upgrade on conversion. Use native melee damage/resistance/knockback; “infected” is not permission to defeat a cosmic superhero by arbitrary true damage. A chosen spitter role is an explicit encounter recipe, not a random upgrade of every newly infected person.

All infection/role state lives outside `def` and saved profiles. Use encounter-only material instances/visible state markers with disposal, not shared roster palette mutation. Native human gait, strikes, falls and ragdoll remain real; do not advertise bespoke zombie locomotion before its moving visual gate. Physical hand attacks can be described as infectious scratches in the prototype, never as a visually nonexistent bite.

### 4. Finite spit must stay finite under native events

Proposed spit: 0.55s interruptible tell, 3s cooldown, 6 toxic direct damage, exposure dose 50, speed 36u/s, **42u total traveled center-path maximum**, radius 0.55u, zero gravity, no initial homing, no splash/pierce/bounce/split/stick/DoT/corrosion/knockback/launch. These are prototype proposals, not final balance. A visible physical mouth/aperture emits the traveling glob after the final animated pose; no hand-punch socket or invisible ray. Local mouth emission must be measured on the supported procedural human head and re-resolved from its live transform; obstruction cancels the prepared shot without firing through cover.

- AI has to see the target, turn its aim, complete the tell and fire at a snapshot of its earned aim. It cannot track an unseen flyer or acquire through cover. A new target does not inherit a completed tell. Broken LOS, hard action interrupt, KO, conversion/reset or removal cancels the pending tell; no wall-clock callback. Source prices/cooldown are paid once at the documented new-source commitment point (proposed tell start); no refund or second payment at launch.
- Tagged direct-only shots bypass only their own explosion/area-damage/civilian-blast response. They still use real native ground/cover/interior/shield/prop/foe ordering. Do **not** set `ballistic:true` just to avoid splash: that invokes armor and civilian blast lanes. Do **not** rely on zero damage/blast/power with current `||` defaults. Respect explicit zero only inside the validated new tagged path; leave legacy defaults unchanged.
- Add tagged shots to `Projectiles.update`'s swept participant predicate explicitly; do not assign combat-interception priority merely to get a sweep. Use `earliestOrdinaryContact` and the same contact commit manager. For each native linear integration segment, clip step time to `min(dt, remaining life, remaining path / segment speed)` **before** testing contacts (zero speed consumes time, not distance); consider the cutoff in the same event-time ordering as cover/foes/expiry. Account only for the distance actually advanced to an earlier contact, then recalculate after deflection. A long frame must not hit behind its range endpoint and then dispose.
- Track immutable launch budget and accumulated actual travel, not distance to a moving caster/target or distance from the current reflected origin. Reflection changes neither budget nor distance already consumed. Native deflection may transfer caster/team and change speed, homing and `life`; tagged travel still ends at the original 42u. Derived safety time is an additional upper bound, not the authority for range. Ground, cover, hit, expiry and range-end all retire mesh/material/audio once without `worldImpact` or an infection radius.
- Finite physical size and the existing generous upright target cylinder matter: the published 42u is a **center-path** cap, not a promise that every target-center at 42.01u is safe. Test the actual nearest target volume and flight height beyond that margin. At equal range event time, preserve native contact tie order; a target touching the final segment can be hit, one only after it cannot.
- Flight stays native. Zombies have flightTier 0, no anti-air teleport, height-scaled spit range, hidden leash damage, compulsory descent or extra inhibitor flight effect. A flyer beyond the actual finite contact envelope is safe even if the spitter can see it. Low flight inside that envelope can honestly be hit. Native damage can still cause its existing, earned stun/KO; do not globally remove that counterplay to promise absolute invulnerability.

### 5. Bounded horde intent, not a second damage system

Proposed first measured crowd: at most **12 live encounter NPCs**, including still-human hosts, with at most **2 spitters**, at most **4 live tagged shots**, and at most **18 total encounter Fighter objects including bodies**. Spawn schedule is finite: 18 participants total, at most 2 new actors/s, no endless replenishment. The limits are proposed engineering gates; lower them if measured native costs require it. They do not prove the eventual ambient crowd scale.

An encounter coordinator makes coarse target/navigation decisions at proposed 5Hz, staggered by actor id, while native movement, melee contacts, projectile sweeps and effects run each normal simulation frame. No collision is sampled at AI frequency. It grants at most 2 simultaneous infectious light-attack starts per target and 1 spit launch per 0.75s across the encounter; waiting actors reposition instead of stacking. State/tells use scaled simulation time and pause with the game. No `Math.random`/timer swarm or unbounded target/path search; use a locally seeded encounter RNG and fixed candidate limits.

Use existing Fighter movement/cover/body collision and honest `canSee`/remembered position principles. A small encounter-only intent branch in `controlBot` consumes `{move, aimDir, aimAt, slots, melee:'light'|null, fly:false}` and routes to native `move`, `runSlot` and `melee.strike` under the native hard action/busy gates. It bypasses normal hero AI's autonomous grab/haymaker/evade/item/counter-beam decisions **only for registered human-infection NPC roles**. Survivor intent flees; infected intent approaches/attacks. Aliens use ordinary hero AI. The same low-level executors remain used by player and bot; no direct `takeDamage` from the coordinator. No new player bindings or BFP camera behavior.

Use bounded obstacle detours with a finite stuck/backoff state; if a building fully blocks the route, wait/search honestly. Do not teleport through geometry or grant omniscient nav. Precise urban pathfinding and large city pursuit are follow-ons if actual moving tests expose a need; no street/map redesign.
