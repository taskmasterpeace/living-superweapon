# Creator direction: visual teaching, readable combat and expandable content

Preserve these September 13 decisions. This is a backlog/design record, not authorization to replace the immediate hand/wrist repair or delay the playable combat foundation.

## Reusable training-poster pipeline (preferred over scripted lessons now)
- One versioned lesson definition: mechanic ID, verified rules, current keyboard/mouse/controller/touch bindings, characters, three steps, outcome/counter, evidence clip, art reference and revision.
- Produce a consistent three-panel poster with our own characters, warm-neutral/gold UI, numbered actions and clear motion arrows. Generate art separately; typeset exact controls from binding data so image generation cannot invent buttons.
- Use approved step-one artwork as a visual reference for subsequent steps. Reuse outputs as wall posters, inspection cards and marketing exports.
- Detect stale controls/rules; posters describe verified gameplay, not aspirations. Keep editable sources, generated images, layout and provenance together.
- Initial posters: approach/active/recovery; punch/block/grab; aerial attack; carry/throw; controlled flight/knockback/uncontrolled falling/KO. Avoid building a scripted tutorial campaign in this pass.

## Presentation extensions
- Thought bubbles distinct from speech; jagged shouting treatment and cooldowns. Telepathy and Sandra's Its Voice reveal only authored knowledge permissions; ordinary speech must not leak hidden enemies.
- Optional angled comic inset for grab/body blow/throw; fixed shot recipes, one bounded secondary view, disable/reduce on performance preset. It must not move the gameplay camera, hide aiming or affect simulation. Prototype before rollout.
- Shared emblem IDs, palettes and region/attachment compatibility across vehicles and characters; preserve appearance parity across selection/editor/gameplay.
- Compact inventory beside the character, class-filtered equipment, actual model/icon/attachment coverage and readable primary/secondary power selection. No generic repeating button wall.

## Content authoring
- Projectile library extends existing ability/beam/visual owners: silhouette, motion, emission, impact, residue and damage type are separate fields. Arrow, magical arrow, ice shard and energy blast need distinct shapes, not just colors. Preview and compare using native trajectories; beams retain traveling tips.
- Ability delivery categories: passive, toggle/sustain, charged, projectile, traveling beam, aimed-point area, targeted effect, movement, summon/construct. Keep delivery separate from damage type and input binding.
- Expand shared punches/kicks/weapons only after contact works; authored timings, reach, recoveries, body/material response, grip/weapon sockets, resource costs and counterplay. Do not grant unconditional knockdowns against every body by animation alone.

## Future campaign, not overnight scope
- Inspect D:/git/ShootEM ATLAS Base Maker / player facility builder: common BasePlan compiler, typed room scales, frontage, egress, furnishings and validation receipts. Confirm runtime compatibility before reuse.
- World/sector ownership, city capture/domination/destruction, recruitable LSWs, printers and recoverable equipment; start with linked tactical sectors before seamless globe travel.
- Specimen identity -> chosen research -> shared team recipe -> per-fighter compatible installation or printed equipment; not random universal powers.
- Battle royale is a later mode; first prove a short repeatable scored match with existing AI and respawns.

## Three favorable next slices
A. Combat Match (recommended): shared hands, Soldier boxing, aerial counterplay, short scored team match/reset, three posters, minimum inventory clarity. Best playable payoff; campaign and wardrobe expansion wait.
B. Expedition: establish base/depot travel, combat, sample return and chosen upgrade/redeploy. Strong purpose and vehicle relevance; more navigation/integration risks.
C. Creator/Arsenal: appearance/emblems, animation grips, inventory and projectile authoring. Strong reusable production payoff; less immediate match fun.
